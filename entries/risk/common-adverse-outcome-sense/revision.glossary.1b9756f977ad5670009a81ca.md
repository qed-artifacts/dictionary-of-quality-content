# Risk

*Scope: common adverse-outcome sense*

Risk is the possibility of an unwanted event or outcome under uncertainty, considered in light of its consequences.

A useful risk statement says what could happen, what would be affected, and which conditions make the outcome more or less likely. Probability and severity can help analysis, but a single score need not capture the whole concern.

Uses of risk differ across fields. This entry names the common adverse-outcome sense; frameworks that include favorable departures require an explicitly broader sense.
