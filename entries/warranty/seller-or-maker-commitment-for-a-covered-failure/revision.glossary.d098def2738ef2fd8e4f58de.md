# Warranty

*Scope: seller or maker commitment for a covered failure*

A warranty is a seller’s or maker’s commitment to provide a stated remedy if a product fails under covered conditions.

Its terms specify what is covered, for how long or under what use conditions, and whether the remedy is repair, replacement, compensation, or another stated response.

A warranty defines an obligation after a covered problem occurs; it does not establish that the product will not fail.
