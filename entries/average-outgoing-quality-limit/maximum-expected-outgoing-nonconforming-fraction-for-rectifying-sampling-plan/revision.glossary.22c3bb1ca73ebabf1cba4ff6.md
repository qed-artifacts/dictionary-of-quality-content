# Average outgoing quality limit

*Scope: maximum expected outgoing nonconforming fraction for rectifying sampling plan*

The average outgoing quality limit (AOQL) is the highest average outgoing quality predicted for a rectifying sampling plan across possible incoming quality levels.

It is the peak of the plan's AOQ curve when rejected lots are inspected and nonconforming units are replaced as assumed.

AOQL describes a maximum long-run expectation under that plan, not a guarantee about every outgoing lot.
