# Relative contribution

*Scope: category share of an observed total in a Pareto analysis*

In a Pareto analysis, relative contribution is the share of the observed total represented by one category.

For counts, divide the category count by the count across all included categories and state the population and period being counted.

A large share identifies where observations concentrate; it does not, by itself, establish the cause of those observations.
