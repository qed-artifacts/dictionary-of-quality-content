# Administrative justice

*Scope: fairness and lawfulness of public-agency decisions affecting people*

Administrative justice concerns whether public agencies make and apply decisions affecting people through fair and lawful procedures.

In the U.S. example, a person facing loss of a benefit or license may have a right to be heard and a route to challenge the agency's decision.

The question is how public power is exercised and reviewed, rather than whether every affected person receives the outcome sought.
