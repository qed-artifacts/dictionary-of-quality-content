# Z-value

*Scope: normal-distribution statistics*

For a normally distributed quantity, a Z-value indicates how many standard deviations an observation lies above or below its mean.

Compute it by subtracting the mean from the observation and dividing by a nonzero standard deviation. Positive values lie above the mean, negative values below it, and zero is at the mean.
