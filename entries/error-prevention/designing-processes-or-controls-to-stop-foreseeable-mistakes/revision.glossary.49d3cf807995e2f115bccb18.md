# Error prevention

*Scope: designing processes or controls to stop foreseeable mistakes*

Error prevention is the use of process or product design to stop a foreseeable mistake before it produces an incorrect result.

A mistake-proofing device may make the wrong action impossible or reveal it in time for correction.

Checking a completed result for defects is a later control and does not, by itself, prevent the original error.
