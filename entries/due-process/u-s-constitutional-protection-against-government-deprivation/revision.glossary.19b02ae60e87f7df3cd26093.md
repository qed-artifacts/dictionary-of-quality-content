# Due process

*Scope: U.S. constitutional protection against government deprivation*

In U.S. constitutional law, due process constrains how government may deprive a person of life, liberty, or property.

It calls for legally fair treatment and can concern both the procedure used and the justification for government action.

The protections that apply depend on the legal setting and the interest at stake; the phrase alone does not specify a single hearing procedure.
