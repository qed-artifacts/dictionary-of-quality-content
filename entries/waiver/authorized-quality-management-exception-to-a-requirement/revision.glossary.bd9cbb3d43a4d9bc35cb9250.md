# Waiver

*Scope: authorized quality-management exception to a requirement*

In quality management, a waiver is an authorized, bounded exception that permits departure from an otherwise applicable requirement.

It should identify who authorized the departure, which product or process it covers, and when or after how much production it expires.

A waiver does not show that the original requirement was met; after it expires, that requirement applies again unless formally changed.
