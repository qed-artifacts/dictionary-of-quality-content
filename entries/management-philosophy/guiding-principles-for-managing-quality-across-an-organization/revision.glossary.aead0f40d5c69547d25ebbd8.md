# Management philosophy

*Scope: guiding principles for managing quality across an organization*

A management philosophy is a set of guiding beliefs about what an organization should pursue and how its people should manage work to pursue it.

In quality management, such a philosophy can shape priorities, leadership practices, employee participation, and the use of evidence for improvement.

A tool or procedure may put one principle into practice, but it is not the whole philosophy.
