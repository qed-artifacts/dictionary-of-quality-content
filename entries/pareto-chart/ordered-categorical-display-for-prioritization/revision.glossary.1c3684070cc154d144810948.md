# Pareto chart

*Scope: ordered categorical display for prioritization*

A Pareto chart displays categories as bars ordered from the largest to the smallest contribution under a stated measure.

The measure may be a count or relative frequency, and an added line may show how contributions accumulate.

The chart helps prioritize what to examine; it does not establish a universal 80/20 split or identify causes.
