# Mean time between failures

*Scope: average operating time per failure in a repairable system*

For a repairable system, mean time between failures (MTBF) is the average operating time per failure over a stated observation period.

It is commonly calculated as total operating time divided by the number of observed failures.

It excludes counted downtime; mean time to repair describes restoration time. The average does not predict when the next failure will occur.
