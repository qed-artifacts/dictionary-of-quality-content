# Visual management

*Scope: visible process signals and response in lean work management*

Visual management uses visible signals and displays to make a process's status, expected condition, and deviations easy for people doing the work to see.

A display may show whether work is on schedule, supplies are available, or an action has an owner.

Its purpose includes timely response when a deviation appears; displaying information without acting on it does not complete that management practice.
