# Legal quality

*Scope: rule-of-law qualities of legal rules and official action*

In a rule-of-law assessment, legal quality concerns how well rules and official acts are publicly knowable, coherent, and capable of guiding conduct without arbitrary exercise of power.

These formal qualities matter, but they do not alone settle whether a law is just; substantive accounts also examine rights and equality.
