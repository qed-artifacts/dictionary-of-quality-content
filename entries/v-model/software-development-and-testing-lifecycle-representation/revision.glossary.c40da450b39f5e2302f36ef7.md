# V-model

*Scope: software development and testing lifecycle representation*

The V-model is a software-development framework that pairs successive requirements and design stages with corresponding integration and testing activities.

One side develops the system from requirements toward implementation; the other checks components, their integration, the whole system, and its fit for users.

Passing a component test alone does not establish that the assembled system meets its intended use.
