# Conformity

*Scope: fulfillment of an applicable requirement*

Conformity is the condition of meeting an applicable requirement.

A claim of conformity should say which object and requirement are involved. When several requirements apply, identify the set covered by the claim and how fulfillment was established.

Conformity to a requirement does not guarantee suitability for every use. In many contexts, conformance names the same relationship.
