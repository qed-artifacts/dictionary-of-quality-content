# Food safety

*Scope: adverse-health-effect assurance for food used as intended*

Food safety is warranted confidence that a food will not cause adverse health effects when it is prepared or eaten as intended.

The intended use and the consumers matter when identifying relevant hazards and controls.

A hygiene program or hazard-control plan can support food safety, but the plan is not itself proof that a particular food is safe.
