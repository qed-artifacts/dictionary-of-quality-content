# Lean Six Sigma

*Scope: combined Lean and Six Sigma approach to improving processes*

Lean Six Sigma is a process-improvement approach combining Lean's attention to waste and flow with Six Sigma's attention to variation and defects.

Teams use evidence about customer needs and process performance to select and test changes.

The combined name describes an approach, not a fixed list of tools or a guarantee of a particular result.
