# Rolled throughput yield

*Scope: share or estimated probability of passing all process steps without defects or rework*

Rolled throughput yield is the proportion, or estimated probability, of units that pass every step of a defined process flow without a defect or rework.

For consistently defined step-level first-pass yields, multiply the step yields to estimate the chance of first-pass success through the full flow. State the steps and counting rules.

This differs from final yield: a process can deliver many acceptable units after repair while having a much lower rolled throughput yield.
