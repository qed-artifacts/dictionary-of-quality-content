# Tampering

*Scope: overadjustment of a stable process in statistical process control*

In statistical process control, tampering is adjusting a stable process in response to ordinary variation as though an unusual cause had appeared.

Such adjustments can increase variation even when intended to move the next result toward a target.

A control chart can help distinguish expected variation from a signal that warrants investigation before changing the process.
