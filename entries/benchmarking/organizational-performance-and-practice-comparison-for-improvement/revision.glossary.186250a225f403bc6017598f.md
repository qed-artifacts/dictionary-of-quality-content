# Benchmarking

*Scope: organizational performance and practice comparison for improvement*

Benchmarking is a planned comparison of an organization's practice or performance with a relevant reference to identify differences and possible improvements.

The reference may be another organization or an appropriate practice or performance level; the basis of comparison should be stated.

A measured gap helps focus inquiry, but it does not show by itself why the gap exists or which change will close it.
