# Continuity of care

*Scope: coherence of a patient's care across time and settings*

Continuity of care is the extent to which a person's care remains connected and coherent over time and across providers or settings.

Relevant information and plans should carry across transitions so that each episode can build on what came before.

Coordination is work used to achieve continuity; continuity describes a quality of the care the person receives.
