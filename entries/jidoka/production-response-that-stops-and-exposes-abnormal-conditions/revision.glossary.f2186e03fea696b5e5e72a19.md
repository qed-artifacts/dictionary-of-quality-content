# Jidoka

*Scope: production response that stops and exposes abnormal conditions*

In production, jidoka is a practice of stopping work when an abnormal or defective condition appears so it can be addressed before production continues.

A machine or worker may trigger the stop, making the condition visible for investigation.

It intervenes at the point of production rather than relying only on inspection after output has moved on.
