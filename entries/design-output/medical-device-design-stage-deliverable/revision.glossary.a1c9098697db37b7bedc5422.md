# Design output

*Scope: medical-device design-stage deliverable*

In medical-device design, a design output is a deliverable from a design stage that expresses the device design or how it will be made and checked.

It may be a specification, drawing, manufacturing instruction, or test protocol. An output from one stage can become an input to the next.

Teams relate outputs to relevant design inputs and verify them against applicable requirements; producing a document alone does not show that those requirements are met.
