# Response surface

*Scope: modeled expected response over combinations of input levels*

A response surface represents how the expected value of a response varies across combinations of two or more input variables.

It may be expressed as a model and shown as a three-dimensional surface or contour plot. Such views can help locate promising operating conditions.

A fitted surface estimates behavior in a studied region; predictions beyond that region or claims of an optimum require further justification.
