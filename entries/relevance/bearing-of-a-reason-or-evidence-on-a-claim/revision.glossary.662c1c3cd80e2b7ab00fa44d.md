# Relevance

*Scope: bearing of a reason or evidence on a claim*

In an argument, relevance is the bearing a reason or piece of evidence has on the particular claim it is offered to support.

A fact can be accurate yet irrelevant if it does not help establish the claim at issue.

Relevance is distinct from sufficiency: a reason may bear on a claim without providing enough support to settle it.
