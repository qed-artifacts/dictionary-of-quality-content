# Evidence hierarchy

*Scope: ranking evidence designs for a specified kind of inference*

An evidence hierarchy ranks types of evidence by their expected ability to support a specified kind of claim.

For a question about intervention effects, study design can provide an initial ordering of evidence types.

The quality of the actual evidence also depends on limitations, relevance, consistency, and precision; the design label alone does not settle its strength.
