# Quality dimension

*Scope: aspect of a quality profile*

A quality dimension is a distinguishable aspect under which related quality concerns are organized for describing or assessing an object.

For data, accuracy and completeness are possible dimensions; another domain may choose a different set. Name the object and framework before comparing dimension results.

The dimension is an organizing aspect, not a single observation, measure, or required performance level. These may be attached to it separately.
