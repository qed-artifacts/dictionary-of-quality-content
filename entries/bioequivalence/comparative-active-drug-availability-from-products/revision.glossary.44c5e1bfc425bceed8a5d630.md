# Bioequivalence

*Scope: comparative active-drug availability from products*

Bioequivalence is a supported finding that two pharmaceutical equivalents or alternatives make the relevant active ingredient or active moiety available at the site of action at comparable rates and extents, when tested at the same molar dose under similar conditions.

The products may differ in inactive ingredients; the comparison concerns drug availability, not every product characteristic.

Specific rules can allow certain intentional rate differences. The study method and decision criteria depend on the products.
