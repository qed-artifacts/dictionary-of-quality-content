# Measurement invariance

*Scope: psychometric comparability across linguistic or cultural groups*

In psychometrics, measurement invariance is the extent to which a test measures the intended construct comparably across specified groups.

It matters when scores from different linguistic or cultural groups are compared.

A faithful translation alone does not establish that scores have the same meaning; evidence of comparability is needed for the intended comparison.
