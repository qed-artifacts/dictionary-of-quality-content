# PDCA cycle

*Scope: Plan–Do–Check–Act improvement loop*

The PDCA cycle is a repeatable improvement method: plan a change, do it, check the results against the aim, and act on what was learned.

Planning states the problem, proposed change, and expected result. Doing puts the plan into practice; checking examines what occurred; acting adjusts the plan or the routine before the next cycle.

The four labels do not establish improvement by themselves. The comparison between expected and observed results is what makes the cycle useful for learning.
