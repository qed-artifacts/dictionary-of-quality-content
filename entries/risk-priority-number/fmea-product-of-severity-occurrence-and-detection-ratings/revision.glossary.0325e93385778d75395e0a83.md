# Risk priority number

*Scope: FMEA product of severity, occurrence, and detection ratings*

In failure mode and effects analysis, a risk priority number (RPN) is a score used to help order possible failure modes for attention.

A common calculation multiplies ratings for severity, occurrence, and difficulty of detection under a specified rating scheme.

Because the component ratings are judgments and different combinations can yield the same product, an RPN is a screening aid rather than a direct measure of loss or a universal action threshold.
