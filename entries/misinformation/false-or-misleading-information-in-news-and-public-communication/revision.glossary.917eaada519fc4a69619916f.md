# Misinformation

*Scope: false or misleading information in news and public communication*

Misinformation is false or misleading information presented or circulated as if it were sound, whether or not the person sharing it intends to deceive.

Intentional deception is one route; mistaken or uncritical sharing can also spread it.

Assess the claim against evidence and context rather than relying on a label such as ‘fake news’.
