# Value-stream mapping

*Scope: visual analysis of a defined product or service flow*

Value-stream mapping is a method for drawing the sequence of activities and flows involved in delivering a specified product or service.

A map makes handoffs, waits, repeated work, and other possible sources of waste easier to inspect. It can guide questions about how the current process works and where change may help.

The map represents a defined scope and observation period; it does not prove that every step is wasteful or that a proposed change will improve the result.
