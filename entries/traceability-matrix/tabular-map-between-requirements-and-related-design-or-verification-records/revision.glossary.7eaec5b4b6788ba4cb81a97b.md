# Traceability matrix

*Scope: tabular map between requirements and related design or verification records*

A traceability matrix is a table that records links between requirements and the design, implementation, tests, or other records that address them.

It helps a team check coverage and see what may need review when a requirement or related artifact changes. The rows and columns should identify the linked items and the nature of each link.

A filled cell shows a recorded relationship; it does not by itself prove that the requirement was met. The supporting design or test evidence still needs examination.
