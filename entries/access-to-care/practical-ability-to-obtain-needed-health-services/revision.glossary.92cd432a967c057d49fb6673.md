# Access to care

*Scope: practical ability to obtain needed health services*

In health services, access to care is people's practical ability to obtain needed care when and where they need it.

A nearby facility does not by itself establish access: travel, cost, service arrangements, and social or cultural conditions can affect whether people can use it.

An access judgment should specify the population and type of care rather than assume that everyone near a service has the same opportunity to obtain it.
