# Pharmaceutical quality system

*Scope: ICH Q10 model for pharmaceutical quality management*

A pharmaceutical quality system (PQS) is an organized system for directing and controlling a pharmaceutical organization with respect to quality.

The ICH Q10 model applies across product development, manufacturing, and other lifecycle stages, alongside applicable good manufacturing practice requirements.

It brings together responsibilities and processes for monitoring performance, managing change, corrective and preventive action, and management review.
