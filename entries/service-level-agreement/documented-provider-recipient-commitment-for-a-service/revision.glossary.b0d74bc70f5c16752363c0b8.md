# Service level agreement

*Scope: documented provider-recipient commitment for a service*

A service level agreement (SLA) is a documented agreement between a service provider and a recipient about the service to be provided and the levels of performance expected.

It can specify scope, indicators, reporting, responsibilities, and what happens if an agreed level is missed.

An SLA records a commitment; measured service results show whether that commitment was met.
