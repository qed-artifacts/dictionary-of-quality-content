# Customer interview

*Scope: direct customer conversation to elicit needs and experience*

A customer interview is a direct conversation used to learn a customer’s needs, experiences, expectations, or judgments relevant to a product or service.

An interviewer can ask planned questions and follow up on unexpected answers. The study should identify which customers were approached, the setting, and the purpose of the questions.

An interview provides evidence of what that person reports; general claims about all customers require an appropriate sampling and analysis basis.
