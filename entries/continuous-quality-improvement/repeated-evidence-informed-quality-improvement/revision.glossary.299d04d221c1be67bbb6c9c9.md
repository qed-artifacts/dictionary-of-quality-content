# Continuous quality improvement

*Scope: repeated evidence-informed quality improvement*

Continuous quality improvement is an ongoing, organized effort to improve quality through repeated tests of change and learning from their results.

Teams can plan a change, try it, examine what happened, and decide whether to adapt, adopt, or abandon it.

One successful fix is a change; sustained improvement requires checking what follows and continuing to learn.
