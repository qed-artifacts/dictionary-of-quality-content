# Design review

*Scope: structured examination of a design against requirements*

A design review is a planned, documented examination of a design to judge whether it can meet relevant requirements.

It considers the design as a whole against its intended functions and requirements, bringing unresolved questions into view before a decision to proceed or revise.

Review is an examination and judgment. It should not be confused with a test result or with proof that a finished design will succeed in every use.
