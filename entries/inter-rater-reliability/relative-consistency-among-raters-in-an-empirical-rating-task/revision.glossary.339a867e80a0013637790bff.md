# Inter-rater reliability

*Scope: relative consistency among raters in an empirical rating task*

Inter-rater reliability describes how consistently different raters distinguish among the same targets in a specified rating task.

Raters can rank targets similarly while assigning different values; exact matching concerns inter-rater agreement.

To interpret a result, identify the raters, what they rated, the kind of ratings, and the statistic used.
