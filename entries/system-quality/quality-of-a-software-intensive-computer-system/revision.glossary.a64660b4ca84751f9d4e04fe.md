# System quality

*Scope: quality of a software-intensive computer system*

For a software-intensive computer system, system quality is how well the system meets the relevant needs of its stakeholders.

Assessing it calls for a stated context and relevant characteristics of the system, such as reliability, usability, or security.

The needs of different stakeholders may differ, so one score or characteristic cannot establish the system's overall quality without a stated basis for judgment.
