# Reliability-centered maintenance

*Scope: selection of maintenance actions from functions, failure modes and consequences*

Reliability-centered maintenance is a structured way to choose maintenance actions by examining what an asset must do, how it can fail, and the consequences of those failures.

The operating context, available ways to detect deterioration, and safety and economic consequences shape the choice. An action may be condition-based, scheduled, or another justified response.

It does not mean maintaining every component as often as possible; some failure modes call for redesign or a decision to let a low-consequence item run to failure.
