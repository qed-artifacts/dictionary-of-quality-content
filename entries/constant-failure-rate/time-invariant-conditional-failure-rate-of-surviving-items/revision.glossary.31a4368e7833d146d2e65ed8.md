# Constant failure rate

*Scope: time-invariant conditional failure rate of surviving items*

A constant failure rate is a failure rate that remains the same over a specified interval for items that have survived to each time in that interval.

For items alive at a chosen time origin, if the rate lambda stays constant through the next t units of time, the chance of surviving that interval is e^(-lambda t).

The model does not say that every item fails at the same age; an item's chance of surviving from the start still declines with elapsed time.
