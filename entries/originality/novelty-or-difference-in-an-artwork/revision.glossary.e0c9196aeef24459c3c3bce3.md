# Originality

*Scope: novelty or difference in an artwork*

In art, originality is the extent to which a work differs in significant ways from earlier work or familiar conventions.

It may concern the work's features or how they are combined; difference alone does not establish artistic merit.

Original is also used as praise, so an appraisal should say whether it claims novelty, value, or both.
