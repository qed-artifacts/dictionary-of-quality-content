# Credibility

*Scope: believability of a claim or source in argument evaluation*

In evaluating an argument, credibility is the degree to which a claim or supporting source is believable on relevant grounds.

Those grounds may include the reliability of the data or the relevant expertise behind a claim.

Credible support still has to be relevant and sufficient for the conclusion; credibility alone does not settle the argument.
