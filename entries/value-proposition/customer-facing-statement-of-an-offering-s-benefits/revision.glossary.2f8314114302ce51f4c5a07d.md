# Value proposition

*Scope: customer-facing statement of an offering's benefits*

A value proposition states the benefits an offering promises to a specified customer and why that offering merits consideration against available alternatives.

It should identify the customer, the problem or desired outcome, and the difference the offering is expected to make.

The statement is a claim about prospective value; evidence of use and results is needed to show whether the benefit was realized.
