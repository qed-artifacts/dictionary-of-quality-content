# Quality policy

*Scope: organizational direction on quality*

A quality policy is an organization’s stated direction and commitments concerning quality.

It provides a basis for setting quality objectives and communicating what leaders expect. Its scope and commitments should be clear enough to guide choices about work and resources.

A written policy does not establish that people follow it. Its effect must be judged from decisions, practices, and results in the organization.
