# Autonomy

*Scope: patient decision-making in healthcare ethics*

In healthcare ethics, autonomy is a person's authority to make decisions about their own care in light of their values and informed understanding.

Respecting autonomy calls for information about the proposed care, material risks, and available alternatives before consent is sought.

It concerns the person's decision-making role, not a guarantee that every requested treatment is available or appropriate.
