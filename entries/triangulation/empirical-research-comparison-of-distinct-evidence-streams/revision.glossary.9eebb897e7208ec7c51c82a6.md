# Triangulation

*Scope: empirical-research comparison of distinct evidence streams*

In empirical research, triangulation compares evidence gathered through different methods or observers to examine a question from more than one vantage point.

Agreement can strengthen a finding, while differences may reveal complementary aspects or a disagreement needing explanation.

The term does not require three datasets, and agreement by itself does not guarantee that a conclusion is true.
