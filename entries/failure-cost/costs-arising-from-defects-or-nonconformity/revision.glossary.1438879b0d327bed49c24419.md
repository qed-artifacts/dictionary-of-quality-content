# Failure cost

*Scope: costs arising from defects or nonconformity*

A failure cost is a cost that arises because a product, service, or process fails to meet a relevant quality requirement.

Internal failure costs arise before delivery, such as scrap or rework. External failure costs arise during or after delivery, such as returns, warranty work, or complaint handling.

The estimate depends on the accounting boundary: some consequences, including lost trust or delayed work, can be difficult to attribute or price. Prevention and appraisal costs belong to different categories.
