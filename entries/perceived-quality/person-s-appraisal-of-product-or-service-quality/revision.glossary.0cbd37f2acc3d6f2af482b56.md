# Perceived quality

*Scope: person's appraisal of product or service quality*

Perceived quality is a person's appraisal of how good a product or service seems for a relevant purpose.

The appraisal depends on the person's experience and judgment about the product or service and may differ across users or uses.

It is an assessment made by someone, not a direct measurement of every characteristic or proof of actual performance.
