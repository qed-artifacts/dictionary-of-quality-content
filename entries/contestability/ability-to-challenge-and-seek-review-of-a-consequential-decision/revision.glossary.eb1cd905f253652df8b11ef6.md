# Contestability

*Scope: ability to challenge and seek review of a consequential decision*

Contestability is the practical ability to challenge a decision, claim, or system output and seek a reasoned response.

For consequential decisions, it depends on knowing what was decided, having a usable way to raise a challenge, and receiving a review capable of addressing it.

A formal appeal is one way to provide contestability, but the opportunity to question and correct an output can exist outside an appeal process.
