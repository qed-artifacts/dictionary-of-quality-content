# Construct validity

*Scope: test-score interpretation concerning an intended construct*

Construct validity concerns how well evidence supports interpreting test scores as reflecting a specified construct for a stated population and use.

The evidence can address the test's content, the behavior of scores, and their relationships with other variables.

Validity belongs to the proposed interpretation and use of the scores; it is not a permanent certificate attached to the test.
