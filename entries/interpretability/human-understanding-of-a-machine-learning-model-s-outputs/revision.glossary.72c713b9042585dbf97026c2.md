# Interpretability

*Scope: human understanding of a machine-learning model's outputs*

In machine learning, interpretability is the extent to which a person can understand how a model reaches or supports an output.

A short decision path may let a reader trace a classification through visible tests; a more complex model may be harder to inspect.

Interpretability depends on the question and audience and does not by itself establish that a model's output is correct.
