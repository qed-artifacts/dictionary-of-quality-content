# Management response

*Scope: management action or plan in answer to an audit finding*

In an audit, a management response is the audited organization’s account of action taken or planned in answer to a finding.

An immediate correction may address a visible problem without eliminating its cause.

For a nonconformity, the response should connect the finding to cause analysis and corrective action; follow-up evidence is needed to establish whether the action was effective.
