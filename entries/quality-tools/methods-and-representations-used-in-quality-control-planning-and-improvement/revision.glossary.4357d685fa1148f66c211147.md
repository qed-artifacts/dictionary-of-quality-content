# Quality tools

*Scope: methods and representations used in quality control, planning and improvement*

Quality tools are methods and representations used to examine, plan, control, or improve aspects of quality.

Examples include a check sheet for collecting observations, a histogram for displaying their distribution, and a cause-and-effect diagram for organizing possible causes.

The seven basic quality-control tools are one named set; planning and management use additional tools.
