# Item difficulty

*Scope: difficulty of a scored test item for examinees*

In testing, item difficulty describes how challenging a particular question is for the examinees or ability range of interest.

In classical item analysis, it is often reported as the proportion who answer correctly, so a higher reported proportion means an easier item.

In item response theory, a difficulty parameter locates the item on the modeled ability scale; the two quantities should not be confused.
