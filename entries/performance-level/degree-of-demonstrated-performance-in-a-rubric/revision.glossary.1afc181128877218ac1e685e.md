# Performance level

*Scope: degree of demonstrated performance in a rubric*

In a rubric, a performance level is a named or numbered category describing a degree of demonstrated performance on a criterion.

Descriptions identify features associated with each level; example responses can help raters apply the categories consistently.

Assigning a score or rating places a particular response at a level. The level itself is the category, not the evidence that a particular person attained it.
