# Legitimacy

*Scope: justification of an intervention's framing to affected people*

In participatory systems work, legitimacy concerns whether an intervention's aims, boundaries, and decisions can be justified to people affected by it.

Ask who sets the aims, whose interests count, and who can question the framing.

Technical expertise or a desired result alone does not settle whether the affected parties were treated justifiably.
