# Craftsmanship

*Scope: quality of skilled making in relation to an object's form and use*

Craftsmanship is the quality of skilled making shown in how an object's materials, construction, and details serve its intended form and use.

It can matter in ordinary functional objects as well as art, with practical and aesthetic merits considered together.

In a vehicle, fit, finish, tactile feel, sound, and ease of use can shape a judgment of craftsmanship.
