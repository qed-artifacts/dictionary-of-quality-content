# Research integrity

*Scope: responsible conduct and reporting of research*

Research integrity is responsible conduct and truthful reporting of inquiry so that its methods, evidence, and conclusions can be trusted.

It calls for sound methods, honest handling of data, and transparency about relevant limitations.

When people take part in research, respecting their rights and welfare is also part of responsible conduct; avoiding fabrication alone is insufficient.
