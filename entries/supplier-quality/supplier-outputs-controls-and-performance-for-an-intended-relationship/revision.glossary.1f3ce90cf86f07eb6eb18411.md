# Supplier quality

*Scope: supplier outputs, controls and performance for an intended relationship*

Supplier quality is how well a supplier’s outputs and relevant processes meet the requirements of an intended customer relationship.

Assessment may consider delivered conformity, consistency, process controls, timeliness, and response when problems occur. The importance of each depends on the purchased item or service and the consequences of failure.

A single delivery score cannot establish the quality of every supplier activity. State the requirements, period, and evidence behind the judgment.
