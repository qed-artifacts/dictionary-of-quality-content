# Epistemic risk

*Scope: risk of false belief from a specified belief-forming basis*

Epistemic risk is the possibility of getting something wrong in forming or relying on a belief.

The relevant risk depends on the claim and the way the belief is formed: a belief may happen to be true even though its basis could easily have led to error.

In this sense, reducing risk means seeking more dependable ways of arriving at the truth, not merely counting how often a belief turns out true.
