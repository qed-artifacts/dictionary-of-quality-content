# Statistical significance

*Scope: null-hypothesis test decision under a stated threshold*

A result is statistically significant under a specified hypothesis test when its data meet the stated rule for rejecting the null hypothesis.

A common rule compares the p-value with a chosen significance level, such as 0.05, under the test's assumptions.

Statistical significance does not establish that an effect is large, important in practice, or caused by the factor under study.
