# Procedural fairness

*Scope: fairness in making a public decision*

In public decision-making, procedural fairness is fairness in how a decision is reached, including a meaningful opportunity for affected people to be heard and have their views considered.

It concerns the decision process; whether the outcome favors a particular person is a separate question.

Timely decisions and clear procedures can matter when delay or uncertainty would undermine that opportunity.
