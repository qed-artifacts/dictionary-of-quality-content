# Confidence

*Scope: strength of trust in an evidence-based estimate or conclusion*

In evidence appraisal, confidence is the degree of trust placed in a specified estimate or conclusion given the available evidence and its limitations.

It is tied to the outcome and intended use; the same studies may support different levels of confidence for different outcomes.

An evidence-confidence judgment is not the same as a statistical confidence interval or the strength of a recommendation.
