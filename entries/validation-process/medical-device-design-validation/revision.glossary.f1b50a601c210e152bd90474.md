# Validation process

*Scope: medical-device design validation*

In medical-device design, a validation process is a planned sequence for gathering and reviewing evidence that the device design meets user needs and its intended uses.

It identifies intended uses, methods, and acceptance criteria, then records results and resolves discrepancies.

Verification against design inputs can contribute evidence, but it does not by itself show that the design meets user needs.
