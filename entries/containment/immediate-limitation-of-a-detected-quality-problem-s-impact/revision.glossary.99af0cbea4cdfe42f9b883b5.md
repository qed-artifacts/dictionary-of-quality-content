# Containment

*Scope: immediate limitation of a detected quality problem's impact*

In quality problem response, containment is immediate action to limit the reach or impact of a detected defect or nonconformity.

It may involve identifying affected units, segregating them, or preventing further use or delivery while the issue is assessed.

Containment protects against further effects; corrective action addresses causes so the problem is less likely to recur.
