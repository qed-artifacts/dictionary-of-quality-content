# Security

*Scope: protection and authorized use of organizational information*

In information management, security is the protection of information from inappropriate access, use, alteration, or loss while allowing authorized use.

Controls depend on what information needs protection, who may use it, and the applicable privacy, contractual, and business requirements.

Restricting all access would defeat legitimate use; security must protect the information while permitting the right people to work with it.
