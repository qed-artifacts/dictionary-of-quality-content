# Level of analysis

*Scope: scale of units addressed by observations and claims in research*

In research, a level of analysis specifies the kind or scale of unit about which observations and claims are organized, such as an individual, household, organization, or nation.

It helps a reader tell whether a reported pattern concerns people separately or aggregates such as cities or organizations.

A pattern observed for groups does not by itself establish the same pattern for every member of those groups.
