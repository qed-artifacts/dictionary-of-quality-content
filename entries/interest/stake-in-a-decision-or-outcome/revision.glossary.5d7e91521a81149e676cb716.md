# Interest

*Scope: stake in a decision or outcome*

In a decision, an interest is something a person or group has at stake because the outcome may affect what matters to them.

The interest may concern a possible benefit, burden, valued condition, or protected claim; identify whose interest it is and how it may be affected.

Being interested in an issue does not itself give someone formal authority to decide it.
