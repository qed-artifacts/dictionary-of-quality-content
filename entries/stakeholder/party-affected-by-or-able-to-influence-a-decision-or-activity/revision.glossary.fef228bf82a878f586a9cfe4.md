# Stakeholder

*Scope: party affected by or able to influence a decision or activity*

A stakeholder is a person, group, or organization that can affect, be affected by, or reasonably see itself as affected by a decision or activity.

Stakeholders may include users, workers, suppliers, regulators, and others beyond the immediate customer. Which ones matter for a particular judgment depends on the decision and its likely effects.

Identify whose interests are at stake and how they may be affected. Being a stakeholder does not mean having the same role, authority, or claim as every other stakeholder.
