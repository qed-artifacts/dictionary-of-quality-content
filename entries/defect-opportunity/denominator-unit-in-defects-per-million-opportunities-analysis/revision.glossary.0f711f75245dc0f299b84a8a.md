# Defect opportunity

*Scope: denominator unit in defects-per-million-opportunities analysis*

A defect opportunity is one specified chance for a unit to fail a stated requirement.

A unit may have several opportunities, such as distinct required features or checks. Count them consistently before calculating defects per million opportunities.

An opportunity is a possible failure point; a defect is a failure actually found.
