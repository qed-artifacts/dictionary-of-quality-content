# Measurement

*Scope: metrology measurement process*

Measurement uses a defined empirical procedure to obtain a value or set of values for a specified quantity.

The procedure involves comparison or counting and may include calculations to obtain the result.

It requires a clear account of what is being measured and how.

A measurement result reports the attributed value or values with relevant information, generally including measurement uncertainty. Deciding whether it satisfies a requirement is a further step.
