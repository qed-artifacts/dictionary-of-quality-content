# Quality gap

*Scope: SERVQUAL service-quality assessment*

In service-quality assessment, a quality gap is a mismatch between what customers expect and what they perceive a service delivered.

The SERVQUAL framework compares expectation and performance ratings and also identifies organizational gaps that may contribute to the mismatch. A difference score indicates a discrepancy; it does not by itself establish its cause.
