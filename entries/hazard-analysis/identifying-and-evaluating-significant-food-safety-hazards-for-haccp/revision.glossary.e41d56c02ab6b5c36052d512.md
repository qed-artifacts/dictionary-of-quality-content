# Hazard analysis

*Scope: identifying and evaluating significant food-safety hazards for HACCP*

In food safety, hazard analysis is a systematic examination of potential hazards in a food operation to identify which need control.

It considers the steps in the operation, the significance of possible hazards, and measures that could control them.

The analysis informs a hazard-control plan; it does not by itself show that controls were implemented or food is safe.
