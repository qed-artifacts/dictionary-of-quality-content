# Public interest

*Scope: journalistic justification involving shared civic concerns*

In journalism, the public interest is the benefit to a community from information that helps people understand and judge matters affecting their shared life.

It can justify investigating or publishing a matter, but the claim should identify whose interests are served and how.

Audience curiosity or likely popularity alone does not establish that a story serves the public interest.
