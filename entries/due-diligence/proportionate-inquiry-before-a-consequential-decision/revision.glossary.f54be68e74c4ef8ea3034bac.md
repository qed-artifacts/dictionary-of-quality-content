# Due diligence

*Scope: proportionate inquiry before a consequential decision*

Due diligence is a reasonable investigation of relevant facts and risks before making a consequential decision or taking action.

What counts as reasonable depends on the decision, the available information, and the duties involved.

In a transaction, the inquiry may reveal both reasons to withdraw and opportunities to pursue; it does not require exhaustive knowledge.
