# Mean time to failure

*Scope: average first-failure time for items treated as nonrepairable*

For items treated as nonrepairable, mean time to failure (MTTF) is the average operating time until first failure.

It may be estimated from observed lifetimes or calculated from a fitted lifetime distribution.

The reciprocal of a failure rate gives MTTF only under a constant-rate exponential model; MTBF instead summarizes operation between failures of a repairable system.
