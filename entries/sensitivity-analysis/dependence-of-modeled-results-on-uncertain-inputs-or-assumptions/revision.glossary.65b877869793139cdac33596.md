# Sensitivity analysis

*Scope: dependence of modeled results on uncertain inputs or assumptions*

Sensitivity analysis examines how an analysis's results or conclusions change when specified inputs or assumptions are varied.

State which inputs vary, the ranges or scenarios used, and the outputs compared.

It reveals dependence on those choices but does not, by itself, establish that the model or chosen ranges are correct.
