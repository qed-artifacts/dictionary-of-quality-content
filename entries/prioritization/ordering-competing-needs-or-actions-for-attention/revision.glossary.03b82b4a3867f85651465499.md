# Prioritization

*Scope: ordering competing needs or actions for attention*

Prioritization is deciding which competing needs, options, or actions deserve attention first under stated aims and constraints.

A priority can reflect importance, expected benefit, urgency, feasibility, or a combination whose basis should be made clear. A numerical weight is one possible aid, not the meaning of priority itself.

In a House of Quality, teams may rank customer needs and proposed technical responses. The ranking guides planning; it does not prove that the chosen response will work.
