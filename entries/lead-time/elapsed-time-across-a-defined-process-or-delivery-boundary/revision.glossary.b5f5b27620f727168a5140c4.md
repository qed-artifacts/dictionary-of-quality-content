# Lead time

*Scope: elapsed time across a defined process or delivery boundary*

Lead time is the elapsed time from a specified starting event to completion of a process or delivery of its output.

The start and end points should be stated, such as receipt of an order and delivery to a customer. Time spent waiting within those boundaries counts as well as processing time.

Making one activity faster may leave total lead time unchanged if queues or other delays remain.
