# Measurement quality

*Scope: fitness of a measurement system and result for intended use*

Measurement quality is how well a measurement system and its results serve a stated measurement purpose.

Assess it against the intended measurand, method, conditions, and data requirements, including the uncertainty relevant to the decision.

A repeatable number may still be unfit if it addresses the wrong quantity or is too uncertain for the intended use.
