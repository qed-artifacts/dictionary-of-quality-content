# Quality objective

*Scope: organizational intended quality result*

A quality objective is a result an organization intends to achieve concerning quality.

It gives practical direction to a quality policy. A useful objective identifies the subject, desired result, responsibility, and evidence by which progress will be assessed.

An objective states an aim; an indicator or measure tracks progress, and a target can state the level sought by a particular time. The existence of the objective does not show that the result was achieved.
