# Customer satisfaction

*Scope: customer appraisal of an offering or experience*

Customer satisfaction is a customer’s appraisal of how well a product, service, or experience meets relevant needs and expectations.

It is tied to a particular customer, offering, and time. Expectations and perceptions can change, so a satisfaction result should state who was asked and what experience they judged.

Satisfaction is informative about the customer’s view, but it does not by itself establish conformity to every requirement or the absence of harm to others.
