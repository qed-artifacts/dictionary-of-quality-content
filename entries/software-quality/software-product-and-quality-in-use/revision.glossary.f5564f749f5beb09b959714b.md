# Software quality

*Scope: software product and quality in use*

Software quality is how well a software product satisfies relevant stakeholder needs and requirements in its intended contexts of use.

Product properties can include functional suitability, reliability, security, usability, and maintainability. Which properties matter, and to whom, should be specified for the system being judged.

Quality in use concerns the outcomes of interaction in a particular context. It is related to, but distinct from, a list of product properties or the quality of the development process.
