# Quality Enhancement Plan (QEP)

*Scope: SACSCOC higher-education accreditation, 2024 edition*

Under SACSCOC's 2024 accreditation framework, a Quality Enhancement Plan is an institution's focused plan to improve specified student learning or success outcomes as part of reaffirmation review.

The topic arises from institutional planning and requires broad support, committed resources, and a way to assess results. The term here names a particular accreditation instrument, not every plan for improving educational quality.
