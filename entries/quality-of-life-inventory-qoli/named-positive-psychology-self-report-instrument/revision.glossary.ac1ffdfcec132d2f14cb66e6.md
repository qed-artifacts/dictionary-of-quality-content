# Quality of Life Inventory (QOLI)

*Scope: named positive-psychology self-report instrument*

The Quality of Life Inventory (QOLI) is a self-report instrument that combines how important life areas are to a person with how satisfied the person is in those areas.

It produces an individualized profile of perceived life satisfaction. Its named instrument and scoring method should not be confused with every measure of quality of life.
