# Retention

*Scope: student continuation through a defined interval in higher education*

In higher education, retention is the continuation of students in a program or institution through a specified period or checkpoint.

A retention rate compares the number still enrolled at the end of a defined interval with the number in the starting group.

State the cohort, interval, and whether continuation means staying in the same program or institution; graduation and transfer may be treated separately.
