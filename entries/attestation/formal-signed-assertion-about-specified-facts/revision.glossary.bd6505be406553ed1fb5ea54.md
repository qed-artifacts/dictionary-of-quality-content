# Attestation

*Scope: formal signed assertion about specified facts*

An attestation is a formal assertion by an identified person or body that specified facts or conditions hold.

It should make clear who is making the assertion, what is being asserted, and the scope or time to which it applies.

An attestation records an accountable claim; it does not by itself establish that the claim was independently verified.
