# Concession

*Scope: bounded authorization to use or release a nonconforming output*

In quality management, a concession is permission to use or release a product or service even though it does not meet a specified requirement.

The permission should identify the departure and its limits, such as quantity, time, or intended use.

It authorizes that particular exception; it does not make the product or service conforming or waive other requirements.
