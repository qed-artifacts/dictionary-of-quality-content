# Good manufacturing practice

*Scope: pharmaceutical manufacturing requirements and controls*

In pharmaceuticals, good manufacturing practice (GMP) is the body of applicable requirements and controls for consistently making and handling medicines to required quality standards.

It addresses manufacturing methods, facilities, equipment, and other controls rather than relying only on testing finished products.

The specific obligations depend on the product and jurisdiction; GMP is a practice framework, not a guarantee that every unit is free of defects.
