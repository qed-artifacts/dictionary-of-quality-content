# Risk threshold

*Scope: organizational risk decision rule*

A risk threshold is a specified boundary in a risk scale or rule at which the interpretation of a risk or the required response changes.

Its meaning depends on what risk is assessed, how it is assessed, the direction of comparison, and the action assigned to each side of the boundary.

No single cutoff applies to every product, activity, or setting.
