# Detection

*Scope: FMEA estimate of discovering a failure or its cause*

In failure mode and effects analysis, detection is the estimated likelihood that a specified failure or its cause will be discovered at a stated stage.

The estimate depends on what is being sought and which controls or observers could find it. Some schemes assess discovery before delivery; others assess whether a customer will notice the effect.

A detection rating is separate from the failure’s likelihood and severity. State the scale and its direction: on many FMEA scales, a higher detection number means the problem is harder to find.
