# Batch release

*Scope: EU advanced-therapy medicinal product manufacture*

For an advanced-therapy medicinal product under EU GMP, batch release is the controlled step that permits a specified batch to be supplied or used after the required review and certification.

The review covers relevant manufacturing records, tests, deviations, and applicable authorization requirements before a qualified person certifies the batch.

Certification and assigning release status are distinct steps; a finished batch remains under control until the release step is completed.
