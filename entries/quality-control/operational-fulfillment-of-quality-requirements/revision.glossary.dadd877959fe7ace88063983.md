# Quality control

*Scope: operational fulfillment of quality requirements*

Quality control is operational work used to keep processes or outputs aligned with applicable quality requirements.

It can include checking results, finding departures, and adjusting or correcting the work. The applicable requirements and the scope of the check determine what a passing result establishes.

Some control activities also provide evidence for quality assurance. An inspection is one possible control activity, not the whole of quality control.
