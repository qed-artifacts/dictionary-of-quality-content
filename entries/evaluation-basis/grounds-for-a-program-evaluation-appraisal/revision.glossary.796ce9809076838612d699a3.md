# Evaluation basis

*Scope: grounds for a program-evaluation appraisal*

An evaluation basis is the stated set of purposes, criteria, and evidence used to support an appraisal of something being evaluated.

It makes clear what is being judged, why the chosen considerations matter, and what observations support the conclusion.

Different participants may disagree about the criteria or how strongly the evidence supports a rating.
