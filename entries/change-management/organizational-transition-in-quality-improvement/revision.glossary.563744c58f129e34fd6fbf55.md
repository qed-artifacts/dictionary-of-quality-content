# Change management

*Scope: organizational transition in quality improvement*

In organizational improvement, change management is the planned work of helping people and processes move from a present way of operating to an intended one.

It addresses roles, sponsorship, communication, learning, and the practices needed to make the new way of working take hold.

In software configuration management, the same phrase can instead mean formal control of change requests; that narrower sense should be named when intended.
