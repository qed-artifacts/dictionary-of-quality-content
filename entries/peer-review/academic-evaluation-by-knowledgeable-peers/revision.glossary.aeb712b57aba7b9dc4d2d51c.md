# Peer review

*Scope: academic evaluation by knowledgeable peers*

In academic work, peer review is an appraisal of a submission or proposal by people with relevant knowledge in its field.

It is used, for example, in journal publication and research-grant decisions, where reviewers assess the work against pertinent criteria.

Reviewers may differ in which criteria they emphasize and how they combine them; review itself is not a guarantee that the work is sound.
