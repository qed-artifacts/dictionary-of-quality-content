# Quality requirement

*Scope: quality management*

A quality requirement is a need or expectation concerning an object’s quality that is to be fulfilled.

It may concern a characteristic, a level of performance, or the conditions under which the object is used. Like other requirements, it can be stated, generally implied, or obligatory.

Identify the object, the applicable condition, and the basis for deciding fulfillment. The requirement is the expectation; an observed result is evidence about whether it has been met.
