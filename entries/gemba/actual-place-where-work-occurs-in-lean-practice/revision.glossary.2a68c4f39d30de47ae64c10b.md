# Gemba

*Scope: actual place where work occurs in Lean practice*

In Lean practice, gemba is the place where the work being examined actually happens.

It may be a production floor or another work area where a process is carried out.

A gemba walk is a visit to observe conditions and improvement opportunities there; the walk is an activity, while gemba names the place.
