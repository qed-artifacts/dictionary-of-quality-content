# Data cleansing

*Scope: detecting and addressing dataset quality problems*

Data cleansing is the process of finding and addressing errors, missing values, duplicates, and inconsistencies in data for a stated use.

A response may correct, transform, fill, remove, or flag a problematic value according to the available evidence and intended analysis.

The work may recur when later analysis reveals new problems, and changes should be documented so the dataset's history remains traceable.
