# Corrective action

*Scope: addressing causes of a detected nonconformity*

Corrective action addresses the cause of a detected nonconformity or other unwanted situation so it does not recur.

Repairing or replacing one affected item corrects that case. Corrective action changes the condition that produced it; the two may both be needed.

The action should follow an investigation proportionate to the problem. Its effectiveness must be checked against later evidence rather than assumed from implementation alone.
