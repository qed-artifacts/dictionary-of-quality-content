# Review

*Scope: quality-management examination of performance and evidence*

In quality management, a review is a deliberate examination of performance and evidence to judge suitability, effectiveness, and the need for action.

A management review can bring together objectives, indicators, product or process results, and emerging problems.

The scope, reviewer, and decision authority should be specified; conducting a review does not itself mean that the subject passed.
