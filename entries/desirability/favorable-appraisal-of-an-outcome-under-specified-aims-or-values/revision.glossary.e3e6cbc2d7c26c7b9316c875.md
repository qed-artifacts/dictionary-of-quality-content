# Desirability

*Scope: favorable appraisal of an outcome under specified aims or values*

Desirability is how favorable an outcome appears under a person's aims or values.

It can change with whose perspective is taken and which goals or circumstances matter.

Some decision models represent subjective desirability numerically, but a number is not required for an ordinary desirability judgment.
