# Appropriateness

*Scope: fit of clinical care to the patient's condition and evidence*

In clinical care, appropriateness concerns whether a proposed service or intervention fits a patient's condition and circumstances in light of evidence about its likely effects.

The relevant patient group, clinical indication, and available alternatives affect that judgment.

Care can be delivered skillfully yet still be inappropriate if the intervention is not indicated for that patient.
