# Fairness

*Scope: justifiability of treatment, process or outcome*

Fairness is the quality of treatment, a decision process, or an outcome being justifiable under standards relevant to those affected.

In a decision, the procedure and the result can be judged separately. A fair procedure does not guarantee that everyone will regard the outcome as fair, and a favorable outcome does not establish that the procedure was fair.

People’s perceptions of fairness matter for trust and response to a decision. Those perceptions are evidence of reception, not conclusive proof that the decision was justified.
