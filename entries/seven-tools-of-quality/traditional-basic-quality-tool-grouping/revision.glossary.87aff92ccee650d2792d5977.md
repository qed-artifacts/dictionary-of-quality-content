# Seven tools of quality

*Scope: traditional basic quality-tool grouping*

The seven tools of quality are a traditional group of simple methods for displaying process information and investigating quality problems.

Common members include the check sheet, histogram, Pareto diagram, cause-and-effect diagram, scatter diagram, and control chart.

The name identifies a toolkit, not seven required stages; published lists differ over whether the remaining tool is a flowchart, run chart, or another display.
