# Quality of service

*Scope: specified technical characteristics of web-service delivery*

For a web service, quality of service (QoS) describes specified characteristics of its delivery, such as performance, reliability, or accessibility.

A QoS model identifies which characteristics matter and how they are assessed for the service.

Technical QoS results can inform, but do not alone settle, a user's judgment of the wider service experience.
