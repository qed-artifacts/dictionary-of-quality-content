# Aesthetic merit

*Scope: worth of an object considered aesthetically*

Aesthetic merit is the worth attributed to a work, scene, or other object on aesthetic grounds.

A claim of merit should say what features or experience support the appraisal, rather than merely name a descriptive property.

Judgments may differ even among informed appreciators, so disagreement calls for reasons instead of an assumed universal score.
