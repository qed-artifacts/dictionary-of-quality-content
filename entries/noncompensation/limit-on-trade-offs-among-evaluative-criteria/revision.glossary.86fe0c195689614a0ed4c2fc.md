# Noncompensation

*Scope: limit on trade-offs among evaluative criteria*

In a multicriteria judgment, noncompensation means that strength on some criteria cannot fully offset a specified shortfall on another.

A veto threshold is one way to apply such a rule: crossing it can prevent an otherwise favorable overall classification.

The protected criterion, boundary, and authority for the rule must be stated; the label alone does not determine them.
