# Acceptability

*Scope: people's appraisal of how health care treats and involves them*

In health care, acceptability concerns whether people regard the way they are treated and involved in decisions as appropriate.

Respectful treatment and attention to patients' views are central to this appraisal.

Being able to reach a clinician does not, by itself, establish that the care is acceptable to those receiving it.
