# Model quality

*Scope: predictive machine-learning model in intended use*

For a predictive machine-learning model, model quality is how well it meets the requirements of its intended task and use under stated data and operating conditions.

Relevant evidence may include prediction performance, reliability, interpretability, and constraints specific to that use.

A strong test score does not establish continuing quality after the data or operating conditions change.
