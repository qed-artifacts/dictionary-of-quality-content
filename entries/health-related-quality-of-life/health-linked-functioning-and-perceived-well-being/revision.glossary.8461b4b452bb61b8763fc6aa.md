# Health-related quality of life

*Scope: health-linked functioning and perceived well-being*

Health-related quality of life is how a person functions and feels in the physical, mental, and social dimensions of health.

It includes everyday activities and relationships as well as perceived states such as pain, vitality, and emotional distress.

Assessment commonly asks the person directly, because others cannot fully observe their experience of well-being.
