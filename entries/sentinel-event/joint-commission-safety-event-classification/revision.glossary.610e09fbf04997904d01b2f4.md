# Sentinel event

*Scope: Joint Commission safety-event classification*

In Joint Commission usage, a sentinel event is a safety occurrence that meets its serious-harm criteria or is designated for investigation regardless of that outcome.

A patient-reaching event can qualify when it causes death, severe harm of any duration, or permanent harm of any severity, apart from the natural course of illness.

Certain listed events qualify even without these outcomes. They call for prompt investigation and response; reporting them to the Joint Commission is voluntary.
