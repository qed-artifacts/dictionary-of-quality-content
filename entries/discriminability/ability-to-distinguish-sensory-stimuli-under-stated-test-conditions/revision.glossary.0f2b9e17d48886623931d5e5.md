# Discriminability

*Scope: ability to distinguish sensory stimuli under stated test conditions*

In sensory testing, discriminability is how well a person or panel can distinguish specified stimuli under stated conditions.

It depends on the difference between stimuli, background variation, and the testing procedure.

A test that finds no difference has not shown the stimuli are identical; its power and the number of participants matter.
