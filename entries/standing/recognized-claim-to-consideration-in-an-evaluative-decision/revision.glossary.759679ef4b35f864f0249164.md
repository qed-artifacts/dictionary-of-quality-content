# Standing

*Scope: recognized claim to consideration in an evaluative decision*

In an evaluative decision, standing is a recognized basis for a person's or group's interests or claims to be considered.

Being affected by a decision can give someone a reason to be heard, without giving that person sole authority over the result.

Legal standing is a separate, more specific question about who may bring a matter before a court.
