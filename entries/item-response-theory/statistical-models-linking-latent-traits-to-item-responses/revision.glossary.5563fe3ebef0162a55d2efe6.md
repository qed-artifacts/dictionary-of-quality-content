# Item response theory

*Scope: statistical models linking latent traits to item responses*

Item response theory is a family of statistical models that relates a person's unobserved trait level to the probability of particular responses to test or questionnaire items.

The models describe properties of individual items, such as where along the trait scale responses change and how sharply they change.

They can be used for scored questions or rating-scale responses that have no single correct answer.
