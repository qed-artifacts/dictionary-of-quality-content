# Net promoter score

*Scope: recommendation-likelihood survey index*

Net promoter score (NPS) is the percentage of survey respondents rating their likelihood to recommend at 9 or 10, minus the percentage rating it from 0 through 6, on a 0-to-10 scale.

Ratings of 7 or 8 count in the respondent total but in neither percentage being subtracted.

The score summarizes stated willingness to recommend; it does not alone show actual recommendations or overall product or service quality.
