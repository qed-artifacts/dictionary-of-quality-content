# Methodological quality

*Scope: fitness of research methods for warranted conclusions*

A study's methodological quality is how well its design, conduct, and analysis support the conclusions it is used to draw.

Appraisal uses criteria suited to the question and design; in a trial these may include allocation, blinding, and handling of withdrawals.

Clear reporting helps others judge the methods, but it does not by itself remove bias introduced by a weak design.
