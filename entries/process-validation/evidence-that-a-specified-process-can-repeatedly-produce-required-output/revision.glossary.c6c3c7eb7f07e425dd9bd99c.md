# Process validation

*Scope: evidence that a specified process can repeatedly produce required output*

Process validation is the documented evaluation of whether a specified process can consistently produce output meeting its predetermined requirements.

It examines the process, its operating conditions, and evidence from its results. In regulated manufacturing, the evidence is developed and maintained across the process life cycle.

Testing a finished item alone cannot establish that the process will continue to perform as intended; the process and its controls also need examination.
