# Total quality management (TQM)

*Scope: organization-wide quality management approach*

Total quality management (TQM) is an organization-wide approach that makes meeting customer needs and improving work processes a shared, continuing responsibility.

It joins leadership, participation across roles, evidence about performance, and repeated problem solving. Statistical tools can help, but no single tool constitutes TQM.

Calling a program TQM describes an intended approach. Whether it works depends on how its practices are carried out and what results they produce in the organization’s setting.
