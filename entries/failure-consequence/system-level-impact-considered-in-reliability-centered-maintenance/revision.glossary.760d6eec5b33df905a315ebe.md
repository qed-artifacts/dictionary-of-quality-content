# Failure consequence

*Scope: system-level impact considered in reliability-centered maintenance*

In reliability-centered maintenance, a failure consequence is what a failure would mean for the system and affected people beyond the immediate physical effect on a part.

Consequences may include injury, loss of operation, degraded capability, or cost. Their seriousness helps determine which failures call for preventive action.

A failure effect describes what happens; its consequence expresses why that effect matters for use, safety, or other objectives.
