# Decision rule

*Scope: stated procedure turning evidence and criteria into a disposition*

A decision rule specifies how stated evidence and criteria lead to a selection, classification, acceptance, rejection, or other disposition.

A rule may use cutoffs, an order of priorities, a weighted combination, or a treatment of uncertainty. The relevant inputs and conditions must be clear enough to apply it consistently.

Different defensible rules can produce different outcomes from the same evidence. The rule and its authority should therefore be stated before treating a result as a decision.
