# Expectation

*Scope: customer anticipation of a service*

In service quality, an expectation is what a customer anticipates or believes a service will provide or how it will perform.

Some service-quality approaches compare customers' experiences with their expectations; other approaches assess performance without that comparison.

An expectation is not automatically a contractual requirement; identify whose expectation it is and whether it was adopted as a requirement.
