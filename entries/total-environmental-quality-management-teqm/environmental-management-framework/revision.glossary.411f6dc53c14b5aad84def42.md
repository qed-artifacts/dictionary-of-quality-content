# Total environmental quality management (TEQM)

*Scope: environmental-management framework*

Total environmental quality management (TEQM) is an approach that applies organization-wide quality-management thinking to environmental effects.

It places environmental concerns at the center of decisions about activities and their inputs and outputs. The term names a particular framework; it is not a general label for every environmental-management system.
