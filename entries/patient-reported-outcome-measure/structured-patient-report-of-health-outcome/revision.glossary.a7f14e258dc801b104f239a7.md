# Patient-reported outcome measure

*Scope: structured patient report of health outcome*

A patient-reported outcome measure (PROM) is a structured instrument for recording a patient's own report of health or functioning, such as symptoms or ability to carry out daily activities.

It concerns an outcome experienced by the patient, without substituting a clinician's assessment for the patient's response.

Some PROMs apply across conditions; others concern a particular disease, and repeated use can show change over time.
