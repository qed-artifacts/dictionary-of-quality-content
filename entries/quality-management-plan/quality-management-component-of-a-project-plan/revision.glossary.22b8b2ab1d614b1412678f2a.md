# Quality management plan

*Scope: quality-management component of a project plan*

A project quality management plan explains how the project will apply its quality policies, methods, roles, and resources to achieve stated quality objectives.

It connects the objectives to responsibilities, planned activities, and the checks used to manage quality across the project.

The plan governs the project's approach; a product-specific quality plan may set out controls for one deliverable.
