# GxP

*Scope: umbrella shorthand for regulated good-practice families*

GxP is shorthand for families of regulated good practices used in pharmaceutical development, testing, and production.

The x stands for the particular practice area, such as manufacturing, laboratory work, or clinical research.

The abbreviation does not name a single rulebook; the applicable requirements depend on the activity and jurisdiction.
