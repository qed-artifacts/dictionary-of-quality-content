# Disability-adjusted life year

*Scope: population health loss from premature mortality and nonfatal conditions*

A disability-adjusted life year is a unit of health loss combining years lost to premature death with time lived in less than full health, weighted by severity.

DALYs can be added across conditions or populations to summarize a burden of disease.

The result depends on the reference life expectancy and disability weights used; it is not a direct rating of care quality.
