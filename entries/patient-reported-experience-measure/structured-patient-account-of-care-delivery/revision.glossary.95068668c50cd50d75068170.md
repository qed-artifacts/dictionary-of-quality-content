# Patient-reported experience measure

*Scope: structured patient account of care delivery*

A patient-reported experience measure (PREM) is a structured instrument for recording a patient's account of what happened during health care delivery.

It can ask about communication, access, coordination, or respect, and concerns care experience rather than the patient's health outcome.

Responses can reveal problems in a service or care pathway, but their meaning depends on the setting and people surveyed.
