# DMADV

*Scope: five-stage Design for Six Sigma method for new design or major redesign*

DMADV is a Design for Six Sigma method whose stages are Define, Measure, Analyze, Design, and Verify.

It structures the development of a new product or process, or a substantial redesign, around defined needs and a verified design.

It is a design route; DMAIC is the related sequence typically used to improve an existing process.
