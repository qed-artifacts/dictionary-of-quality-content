# Project quality

*Scope: fulfillment of requirements for project work and outputs*

Project quality is how well a project's work and resulting deliverables fulfill the requirements set for them.

A judgment should consider both how the project is carried out and whether its outputs meet the needs they were meant to serve.

Finishing on time or within budget does not, by itself, show that a deliverable meets its quality requirements.
