# Sampling plan

*Scope: lot-acceptance inspection decision*

In acceptance sampling, a sampling plan specifies how a sample from a lot will be inspected and how its results determine whether to accept the lot.

A single-sample plan states a sample size and the maximum number of nonconforming units allowed for acceptance.

Its decision rule carries risks of rejecting a satisfactory lot or accepting an unsatisfactory one.
