# Quality governance

*Scope: organizational authority and oversight for quality*

Quality governance is the arrangement of authority, accountability, oversight, and decision-making through which an organization directs and checks its work on quality.

It identifies who sets direction, who can escalate concerns, who reviews performance, and who decides on action and resources. These responsibilities may be distributed across leaders, functions, and governing bodies.

Management review is one governance mechanism. Governance also depends on whether decisions are carried out and their effects examined; a reporting structure alone does not establish effective oversight.
