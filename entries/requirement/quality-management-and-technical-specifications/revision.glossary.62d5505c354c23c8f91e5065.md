# Requirement

*Scope: quality management and technical specifications*

Requirement is a need or expectation used to specify what an object, process, or action is expected to satisfy.

In quality management, it may be stated, generally implied by established practice, or obligatory; it need not be written down to apply.

To judge whether a requirement is fulfilled, identify what is expected, of what, and under which conditions.

In a technical specification, state it clearly enough to check fulfillment without prescribing a particular solution unless that solution is itself required.
