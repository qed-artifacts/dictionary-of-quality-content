# Quality of life

*Scope: person-centered appraisal of life circumstances*

Quality of life is a person’s appraisal of how life is going in the circumstances and values that matter to them.

Health and functioning may matter, alongside relationships, psychological experience, and features of the surrounding environment. The relevant mix can differ across people and settings.

A clinical measure or an income figure can inform this appraisal, but neither alone stands for the whole of a person’s quality of life.
