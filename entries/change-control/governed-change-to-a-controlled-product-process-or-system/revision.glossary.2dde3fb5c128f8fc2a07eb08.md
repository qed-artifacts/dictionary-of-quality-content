# Change control

*Scope: governed change to a controlled product, process or system*

Change control is a process for identifying a proposed change, assessing its effects, authorizing it, and recording what was done and the resulting state.

The depth of review should fit the change and its potential effects on quality, safety, compliance, and operation. Implementation may require updated procedures, records, or renewed validation.

A record that something changed is not enough: control also requires a decision about whether and how the change may proceed.
