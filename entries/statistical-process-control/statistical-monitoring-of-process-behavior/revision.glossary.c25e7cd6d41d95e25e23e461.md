# Statistical process control (SPC)

*Scope: statistical monitoring of process behavior*

Statistical process control (SPC) uses process data and statistical rules to monitor variation over time and identify signals that warrant investigation.

A control chart is a common SPC tool. It compares observed behavior with limits based on the process, helping distinguish routine variation from evidence of a change or special cause.

A process can be statistically stable yet fail to meet specifications. Stability and capability answer different questions, so an SPC signal is not a pass-or-fail judgment on every output.
