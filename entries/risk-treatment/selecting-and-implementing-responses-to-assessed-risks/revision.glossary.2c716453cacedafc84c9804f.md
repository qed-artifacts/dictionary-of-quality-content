# Risk treatment

*Scope: selecting and implementing responses to assessed risks*

Risk treatment is the selection and implementation of a response to an assessed risk.

Responses can include avoiding an activity, reducing a risk, sharing or transferring it, or knowingly retaining it with a plan.

The choice should account for remaining risk and the costs and effects of the response; treatment does not imply that every risk disappears.
