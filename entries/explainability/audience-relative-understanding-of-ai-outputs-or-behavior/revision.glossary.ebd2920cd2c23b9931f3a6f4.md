# Explainability

*Scope: audience-relative understanding of AI outputs or behavior*

For an AI system, explainability is its capacity to provide information that helps a specified audience understand its outputs or behavior.

An explanation may address the model's general behavior or a particular decision, depending on what its users need to know.

Its usefulness and limitations must be assessed for that audience and purpose; an explanation that sounds plausible is not automatically adequate.
