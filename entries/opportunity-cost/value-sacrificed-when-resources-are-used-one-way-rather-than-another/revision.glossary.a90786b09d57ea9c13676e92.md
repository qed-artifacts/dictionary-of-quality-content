# Opportunity cost

*Scope: value sacrificed when resources are used one way rather than another*

Opportunity cost is the value of the best available alternative forgone when a resource is used for one purpose rather than another.

Using staff time, space, or money for one option can give up benefits that the same resources could have produced elsewhere.

The cost can exist without a cash payment, and its value may be difficult to express precisely in money.
