# Safety

*Scope: control of harmful consequences under stated conditions*

Safety is the condition in which a person, activity, or system is protected from unacceptable harm under specified conditions.

A safety judgment should say which hazards and affected parties matter, and in what setting. It need not imply that all risk has been eliminated.

A system may operate without failure yet still cause harm in normal use. Reliability and safety therefore require related but distinct judgments.
