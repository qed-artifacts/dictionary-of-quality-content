# Independence

*Scope: auditor separation supporting impartial judgment*

In auditing, independence is the auditor's separation from the activity under review and from interests that could compromise impartial judgment.

Practical arrangements should keep auditors free of bias and conflicts of interest; internal auditors should be separate from the function audited where practicable.

Independence supports credible conclusions but does not replace sound evidence or competent analysis.
