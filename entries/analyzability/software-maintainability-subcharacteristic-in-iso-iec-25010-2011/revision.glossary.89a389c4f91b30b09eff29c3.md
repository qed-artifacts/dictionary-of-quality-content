# Analyzability

*Scope: software maintainability subcharacteristic in ISO/IEC 25010:2011*

In software quality, analyzability is how effectively and efficiently maintainers can diagnose deficiencies, locate likely causes, and assess the impact of a proposed change.

Clear structure, diagnostic information, and documentation can help people identify the parts that need modification.

Finding what should change is distinct from making the change safely; the latter also involves modifiability.
