# Customer

*Scope: recipient of a product, service or information in quality management*

In quality management, a customer is a person or organization that receives a product, service, or information from a supplier.

A customer may be external to the supplying organization or an internal recipient of another unit's output.

The end user is the ultimate recipient or user and need not be identical to an intermediate customer.
