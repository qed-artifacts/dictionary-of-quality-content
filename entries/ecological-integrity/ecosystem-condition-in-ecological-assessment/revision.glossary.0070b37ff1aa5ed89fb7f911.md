# Ecological integrity

*Scope: ecosystem condition in ecological assessment*

In ecological assessment, ecological integrity is the condition of an ecosystem whose physical, chemical, and living features continue to support its characteristic communities and processes.

An assessment needs to identify the ecosystem and a suitable reference condition. Relevant features can include species composition, water conditions, habitat continuity, and ecological interactions.

A forest may retain some tree cover while fragmentation limits wildlife movement; area alone does not establish integrity.
