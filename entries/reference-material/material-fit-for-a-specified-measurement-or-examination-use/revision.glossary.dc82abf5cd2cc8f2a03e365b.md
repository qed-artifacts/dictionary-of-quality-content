# Reference material

*Scope: material fit for a specified measurement or examination use*

In metrology, a reference material is a material shown to be sufficiently uniform and stable for specified properties to serve an identified measurement or examination purpose.

It can support calibration, checking a method, assigning values to other materials, or quality control; its usefulness depends on the property and intended use.

A certified reference material additionally has documented property values with stated uncertainty and traceability.
