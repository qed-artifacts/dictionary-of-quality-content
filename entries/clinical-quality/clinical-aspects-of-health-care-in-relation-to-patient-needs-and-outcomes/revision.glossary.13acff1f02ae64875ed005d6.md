# Clinical quality

*Scope: clinical aspects of health care in relation to patient needs and outcomes*

Clinical quality is how well clinical care uses appropriate knowledge and practices to serve patients' health needs and desired outcomes.

It includes attention to effective care and patient safety; an observed outcome alone may not show which aspect of care caused it.

Access and courtesy matter to patients, but they do not by themselves establish clinical quality.
