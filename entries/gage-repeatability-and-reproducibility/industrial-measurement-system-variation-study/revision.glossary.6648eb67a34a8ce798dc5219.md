# Gage repeatability and reproducibility (gage R&R)

*Scope: industrial measurement-system variation study*

A gage repeatability and reproducibility study estimates how much measurement results vary when the same items are measured repeatedly and by different appraisers.

Repeatability concerns variation under repeated use by the same appraiser and equipment. Reproducibility concerns differences associated with appraisers or other stated changes in measurement conditions.

The study helps determine whether measurement variation is small enough for a specified decision. It is one part of measurement system analysis; it does not, by itself, assess bias, drift, or every source of error.
