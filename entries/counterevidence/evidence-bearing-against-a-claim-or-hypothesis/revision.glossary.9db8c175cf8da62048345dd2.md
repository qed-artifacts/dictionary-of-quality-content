# Counterevidence

*Scope: evidence bearing against a claim or hypothesis*

Counterevidence is evidence that bears against a specified claim, hypothesis, or interpretation.

For example, a finding inconsistent with an initial diagnosis can prompt reconsideration.

How much it changes the conclusion depends on its relevance, quality, and relation to the other evidence; one contrary finding need not settle the question.
