# Leadership

*Scope: direction and example in organizational quality management*

In quality management, leadership is the work of setting direction, showing commitment through action, and enabling people to achieve and improve shared results.

Leaders communicate purpose and values, engage the people affected by the work, and create a focus on action and accountability.

A formal title alone does not establish effective leadership; the relevant evidence is what leaders do and what their direction enables.
