# Expressiveness

*Scope: perceived affective qualities of artworks*

In aesthetics, expressiveness is an artwork's capacity to present qualities that viewers or listeners perceive as akin to human feeling.

A painting may seem mournful or music may sound joyful through their perceptible features and the way an audience engages with them.

Philosophers differ about how such qualities arise; an expressive work need not be a direct report of its maker's feelings.
