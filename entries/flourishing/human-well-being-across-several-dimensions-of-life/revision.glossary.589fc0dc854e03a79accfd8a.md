# Flourishing

*Scope: human well-being across several dimensions of life*

In human well-being research, flourishing is living especially well across several meaningful dimensions of life.

Accounts may consider engagement, relationships, purpose, accomplishment, and positive experience, but they do not all use the same dimensions.

Flourishing means more than the absence of illness; its assessment depends on the stated account of well-being.
