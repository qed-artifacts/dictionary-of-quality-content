# Adverse event

*Scope: patient harm associated with healthcare management*

In patient safety, an adverse event is harm caused by health care rather than by the patient's underlying condition alone.

It can arise from diagnosis, treatment, or other care; whether care caused the harm must be assessed rather than assumed from timing alone.

A near miss creates a risk of harm but does not meet this harm-based sense of adverse event.
