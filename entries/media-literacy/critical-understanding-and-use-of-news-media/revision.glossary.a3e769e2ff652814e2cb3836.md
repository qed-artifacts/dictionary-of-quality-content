# Media literacy

*Scope: critical understanding and use of news media*

In journalism, media literacy is the capacity to interpret and critically assess news and related media messages in light of how they are made and presented.

It includes asking who produced a message, what supports it, and whether it presents fact, opinion, or persuasion.

Media literacy also helps people participate in public communication as informed audiences or contributors.
