# Policy

*Scope: standing organizational guide to decisions and action*

In organizational governance, a policy is a standing guide to decisions and action in a defined area.

It sets direction that can be applied across cases, such as how an organization will address quality or safety concerns.

A policy may be written or tacit; its authority and practical effect depend on who adopts it and how it is used.
