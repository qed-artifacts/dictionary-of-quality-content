# Degradation

*Scope: deterioration of an item's performance over life units*

In reliability engineering, degradation is a worsening of an item's performance characteristic over time or use.

A specified threshold may mark when that characteristic counts as failed; degradation can occur before the threshold is reached.

Measurements of its path may support an estimate of remaining useful life, subject to the model and conditions used.
