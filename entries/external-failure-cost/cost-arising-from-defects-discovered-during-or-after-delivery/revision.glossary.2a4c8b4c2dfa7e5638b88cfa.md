# External failure cost

*Scope: cost arising from defects discovered during or after delivery*

An external failure cost is a cost arising from a defect or nonconformity found during or after delivery of a product or service.

Examples include returns, warranty work, complaint handling, and repairs after the customer receives the offering. The cost belongs to the failure category because the problem has occurred.

A cost estimate should state what delivery boundary and consequences it includes. Possible losses of trust or future sales may be relevant, but their monetary attribution needs separate support.
