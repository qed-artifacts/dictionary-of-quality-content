# D-value

*Scope: microbial inactivation and sterilization kinetics*

In microbial inactivation, a D-value is the time needed under specified treatment conditions to reduce a viable microbial population by one decimal order, or 90 percent.

The organism and treatment conditions, including temperature when relevant, must be stated. One D-value reduces an expected population by a factor of ten; it does not mean that every organism has been eliminated.
