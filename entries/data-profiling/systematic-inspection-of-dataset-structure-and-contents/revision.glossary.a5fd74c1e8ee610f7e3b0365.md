# Data profiling

*Scope: systematic inspection of dataset structure and contents*

Data profiling is systematic inspection of a dataset's structure, values, and patterns to understand its contents and identify possible quality issues.

It can summarize data types, value frequencies, and relationships among fields or tables.

Profiling reveals conditions that may need investigation; it describes the data rather than automatically repairing suspect records.
