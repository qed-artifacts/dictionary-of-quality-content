# design science research

*Scope: artifact-centered research approach*

Design science research (DSR) is systematic inquiry that develops or investigates purposeful artifacts in response to a problem or opportunity and seeks warranted knowledge about their design, use, effects, and limits.

Artifacts may include constructs, models, methods, working systems, or organizational arrangements; they need not be physical products.

A DSR study connects a stated problem, relevant prior knowledge, design work, and evaluation suited to its claims. Delivering a useful artifact alone does not show what knowledge the research contributes.

The approach is used in information systems and other applied fields, including operations and project management. Its methods and evaluation criteria vary with the question, setting, and type of artifact.
