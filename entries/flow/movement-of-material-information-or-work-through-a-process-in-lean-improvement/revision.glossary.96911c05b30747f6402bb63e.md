# Flow

*Scope: movement of material, information, or work through a process in lean improvement*

In lean process improvement, flow is the movement of work, material, or information through connected steps toward an intended output.

A flow analysis looks at how each step passes work to the next, including waiting, interruptions, and buildup between steps.

Coordinating the steps with actual demand helps reduce work in process and avoid sending components onward before they are needed.
