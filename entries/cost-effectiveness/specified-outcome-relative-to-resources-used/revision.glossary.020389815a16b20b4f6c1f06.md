# Cost-effectiveness

*Scope: specified outcome relative to resources used*

In program evaluation, cost-effectiveness is the amount of a specified outcome achieved relative to the resources used to obtain it.

It may be expressed as cost per unit of outcome or outcome per unit of cost when comparing alternatives that pursue the same outcome.

The least expensive option is not necessarily the most cost-effective if it produces a smaller effect.
