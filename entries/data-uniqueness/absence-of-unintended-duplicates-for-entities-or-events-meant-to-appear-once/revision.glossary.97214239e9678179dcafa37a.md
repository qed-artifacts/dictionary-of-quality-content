# Data uniqueness

*Scope: absence of unintended duplicates for entities or events meant to appear once*

Data uniqueness is the extent to which an entity or event meant to appear once is represented without unintended duplicate records or identifiers.

The uniqueness rule depends on what is identified: customer IDs may need to be distinct even though different customers can share a name.

Uniqueness checks require a stated entity and comparison rule; repeated values are not automatically errors.
