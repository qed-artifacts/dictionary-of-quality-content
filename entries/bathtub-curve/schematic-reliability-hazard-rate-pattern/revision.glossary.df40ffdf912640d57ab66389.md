# Bathtub curve

*Scope: schematic reliability hazard-rate pattern*

A bathtub curve is a schematic plot of failure rate against age that falls early, stays roughly level for a period, and then rises.

Its three regions are commonly interpreted as early failures, approximately constant-rate failures, and wear-out failures.

It is a possible population pattern, not a rule that every item follows or proof that the changes occur at sharp, universal ages.
