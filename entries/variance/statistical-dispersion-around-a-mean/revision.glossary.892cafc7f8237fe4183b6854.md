# Variance

*Scope: statistical dispersion around a mean*

Variance is a measure of numerical dispersion based on squared deviations from a mean.

For a population, it divides the sum of those squared deviations by the population size. The usual sample variance used to estimate population variance divides by the sample size minus one.

Variance has squared units; standard deviation is its positive square root and uses the original unit. The population or sample basis should be stated when reporting a value.
