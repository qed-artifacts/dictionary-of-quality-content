# Sustainability

*Scope: maintaining valued activity within enduring limits*

Sustainability is the capacity of an activity or system to continue over time without undermining the conditions on which it and others depend.

An assessment should identify what is to continue, over what time horizon, and which ecological, social, or other limits and affected people matter.

Sustainable development asks how present needs can be met while preserving the possibility of meeting future needs. Sustainability claims can be contested when they conceal unequal burdens or treat a single resource measure as the whole judgment.
