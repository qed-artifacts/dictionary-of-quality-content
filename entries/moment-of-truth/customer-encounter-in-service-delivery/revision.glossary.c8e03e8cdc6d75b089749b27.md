# Moment of truth

*Scope: customer encounter in service delivery*

In service management, a moment of truth is an encounter at which a customer experiences a provider's service and forms an impression of it.

It can arise in booking, delivery, or recovery from a problem, rather than only at the main point of service.

Such encounters can influence satisfaction and future choice, though one contact need not determine the whole relationship.
