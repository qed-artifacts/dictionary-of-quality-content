# Perceived value

*Scope: customer appraisal of service benefit relative to effort*

For a service customer, perceived value is an appraisal of the benefits received relative to the effort and costs of obtaining and using the service.

Price is one cost; time, search, and the risk of failure can matter too.

Two customers may judge the same service differently because their needs and circumstances differ.
