# Objectivity

*Scope: journalistic reporting and inquiry*

In journalism, objectivity is a disciplined effort to make reporting answerable to evidence and critical scrutiny rather than a reporter's unchecked preferences.

It involves checking claims, considering counterevidence and relevant perspectives, and correcting mistakes.

It does not require reporters to have no standpoint or guarantee that every account is complete.
