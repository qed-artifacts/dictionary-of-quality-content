# Rework

*Scope: disposition to make a nonconforming output conform*

Rework is action on a nonconforming product or service intended to bring it into conformity with its requirements.

The result must be checked against the applicable requirements before it is treated as conforming.

Repair has a different immediate aim: making the output acceptable for its intended use, which may leave an original requirement unmet.
