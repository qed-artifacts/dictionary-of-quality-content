# Dependability

*Scope: time-related operating quality of an engineered system*

In systems engineering, dependability concerns a system's capacity to provide acceptable service over time under stated conditions.

Depending on the framework, it can bring together reliability, availability, maintainability, and continuity.

A probability of failure-free operation captures only one part of this broader appraisal.
