# Process approach

*Scope: quality management of interrelated work toward intended results*

In quality management, the process approach manages work by identifying processes, their intended results, and how they interact to produce those results.

It follows activities, resources, responsibilities, inputs, and outputs across departmental boundaries, then checks whether the resulting work meets its purpose.

A process map can describe the work; using the approach also requires managing and improving how the processes operate together.
