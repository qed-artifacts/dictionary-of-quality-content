# Risk management

*Scope: organized response to risks affecting objectives*

Risk management is the organized work of identifying, assessing, and responding to risks that could affect stated objectives.

It connects possible events and their consequences to decisions about prevention, reduction, acceptance, or other responses. The response should fit the setting and the significance of the risk.

Risk management continues after an initial decision: responsible people monitor what changes, review whether controls work, and revise actions when needed.
