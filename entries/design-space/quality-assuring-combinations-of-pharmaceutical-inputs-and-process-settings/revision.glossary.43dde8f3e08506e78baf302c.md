# Design space

*Scope: quality-assuring combinations of pharmaceutical inputs and process settings*

In pharmaceutical development, a design space is a justified set of combinations of material properties and process settings within which intended product quality can be achieved.

Its boundaries account for interactions among inputs rather than treating each setting as an independent acceptable range.

Evidence for the space should show that the relevant combinations deliver the intended quality; results outside its boundary cannot be assumed to have the same assurance.
