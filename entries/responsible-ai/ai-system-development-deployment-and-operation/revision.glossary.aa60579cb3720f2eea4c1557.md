# Responsible AI

*Scope: AI-system development, deployment, and operation*

Responsible AI is the practice of developing, deploying, and operating AI systems with explicit attention to their benefits, risks, and effects on people.

It calls for examining data and model limitations, testing relevant performance and fairness, making system behavior understandable where needed, and monitoring effects in use.

The label alone does not show that a system meets any particular requirement; the responsibilities and evidence must be specified.
