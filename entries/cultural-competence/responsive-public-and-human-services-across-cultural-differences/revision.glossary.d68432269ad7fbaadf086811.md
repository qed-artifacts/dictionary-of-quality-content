# Cultural competence

*Scope: responsive public and human services across cultural differences*

Cultural competence is the capacity of a person or organization to provide services respectfully and effectively across relevant cultural and linguistic differences.

It calls for understanding the people served and adapting communication and practice to their needs and circumstances.

A claim of cultural competence should be judged by what the service actually does for those people, not by a training label alone.
