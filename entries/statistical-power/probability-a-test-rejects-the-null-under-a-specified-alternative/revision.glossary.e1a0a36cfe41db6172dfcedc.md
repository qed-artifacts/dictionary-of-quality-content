# Statistical power

*Scope: probability a test rejects the null under a specified alternative*

Statistical power is the probability that a specified test will reject its null hypothesis when a particular alternative is true.

For that alternative, power equals one minus the probability of a Type II error, often written 1 − β.

Power must be considered for a stated effect and test design; a low-power test may miss a difference that matters.
