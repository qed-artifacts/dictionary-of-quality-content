# Assessment

*Scope: purpose-bound examination*

Assessment is the process of gathering and interpreting information about a specified subject for a stated question or decision.

An assessment may compare evidence with requirements, criteria, or a reference, then report findings or a judgment. Its methods and evidential demands depend on its purpose.

The term is used differently across fields. It often overlaps with evaluation; an assessment need not always conclude with an overall appraisal of merit or worth.
