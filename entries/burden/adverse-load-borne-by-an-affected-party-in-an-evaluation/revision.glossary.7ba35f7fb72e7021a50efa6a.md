# Burden

*Scope: adverse load borne by an affected party in an evaluation*

A burden is a cost, effort, loss, or other adverse load borne by someone under an activity or condition.

Identify who bears it, its form, extent, and time period; a burden can be financial or nonfinancial.

A benefit to one group does not erase a burden borne by another.
