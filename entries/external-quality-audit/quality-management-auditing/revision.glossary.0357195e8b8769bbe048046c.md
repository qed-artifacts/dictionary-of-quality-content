# External quality audit

*Scope: quality-management auditing*

An external quality audit is a second- or third-party assessment of an organization's quality-related activities, processes, or system against stated criteria.

A customer may conduct or commission a second-party audit; an independent body may conduct a third-party audit. An organization can outsource its own internal audit without turning it into an external audit.
