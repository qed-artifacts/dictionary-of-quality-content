# Corroboration

*Scope: additional evidence supporting a particular claim*

Corroboration is support for a claim from additional evidence that bears on the same point.

A separate observation, record, method, or witness can strengthen a claim when it agrees on a relevant detail.

Repeating one unsupported account does not create independent corroboration, and corroborating one part of a claim does not prove the rest.
