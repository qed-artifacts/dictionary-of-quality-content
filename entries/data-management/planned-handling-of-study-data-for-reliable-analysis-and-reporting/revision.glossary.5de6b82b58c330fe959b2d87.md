# Data management

*Scope: planned handling of study data for reliable analysis and reporting*

In research, data management is the planned handling of study data through collection, entry, checking, correction, storage, and transfer so they remain usable for analysis and reporting.

A data management plan specifies sources, formats, responsibilities, and quality checks.

Electronic capture can help with those tasks, but it does not by itself guarantee that the underlying data are accurate or complete.
