# Weighting

*Scope: relative influence of components in a combined result*

Weighting assigns different relative influence to components used in a combined result or comparison.

A weight states how much a component contributes under the chosen aggregation rule; it does not make the underlying evidence more trustworthy.

Changing weights can change a ranking, so the choice and its rationale should be visible.
