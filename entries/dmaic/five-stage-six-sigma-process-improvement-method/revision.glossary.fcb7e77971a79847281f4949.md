# DMAIC

*Scope: five-stage Six Sigma process-improvement method*

DMAIC is a structured process-improvement method whose stages are Define, Measure, Analyze, Improve, and Control.

A team frames a problem, establishes a performance baseline, examines causes, tests improvements, and sets up controls to sustain the result.

The stages guide evidence-based work on an existing process; the acronym alone does not establish that a change succeeded.
