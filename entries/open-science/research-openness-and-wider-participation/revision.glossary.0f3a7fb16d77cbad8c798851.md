# Open science

*Scope: research openness and wider participation*

Open science is a set of practices that makes scientific knowledge, tools, and processes more accessible and reusable, while opening research to wider participation within justified limits.

These practices can include sharing publications, data, software, methods, and protocols with enough context to support scrutiny and reuse.

Open access can support scrutiny, while privacy, security, confidentiality, and rights can justify limits on what is shared.
