# Objective evidence

*Scope: checkable data bearing on existence or truth in quality management*

In quality management, objective evidence is checkable information that supports whether a specified condition exists or a claim is true.

It may come from observations, measurements, tests, or records, provided they bear on the matter being checked.

Calling evidence objective does not make its interpretation automatic; the claim and the way the information was obtained still matter.
