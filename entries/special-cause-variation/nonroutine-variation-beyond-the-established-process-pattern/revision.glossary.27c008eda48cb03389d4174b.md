# Special-cause variation

*Scope: nonroutine variation beyond the established process pattern*

Special-cause variation is variation associated with a specific, nonroutine influence outside a process’s usual pattern.

A suitable control-chart signal can prompt investigation of such an influence. The signal is evidence to examine, not identification of the cause by itself.

A special cause may be removable, but first establish what changed and whether the observed departure reflects a real change rather than a false alarm.
