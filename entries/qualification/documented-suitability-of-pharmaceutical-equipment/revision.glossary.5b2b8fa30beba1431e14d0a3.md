# Qualification

*Scope: documented suitability of pharmaceutical equipment*

In pharmaceutical manufacturing, equipment qualification is the documented demonstration that equipment or a system is suitable for its intended use under stated conditions.

It can include checks of design, installation, operation, and performance according to the equipment's role and the qualification plan.

Equipment qualification supports process validation but does not by itself establish that the manufacturing process is reproducible.
