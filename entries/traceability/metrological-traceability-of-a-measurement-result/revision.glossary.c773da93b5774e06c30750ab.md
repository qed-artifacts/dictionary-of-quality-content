# Traceability

*Scope: metrological traceability of a measurement result*

In metrology, traceability is the property of a measurement result that allows it to be related to a stated reference through a documented chain of comparisons.

Each link in the chain contributes uncertainty that must be accounted for.

Traceability identifies how a result is connected to a reference; it does not alone show that the result is sufficiently accurate for a particular use.
