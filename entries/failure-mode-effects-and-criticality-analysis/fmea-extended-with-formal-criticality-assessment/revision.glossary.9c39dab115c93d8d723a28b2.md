# Failure mode, effects, and criticality analysis

*Scope: FMEA extended with formal criticality assessment*

Failure mode, effects, and criticality analysis (FMECA) is a failure mode and effects analysis that also assesses the criticality of the identified failure modes.

The analysis examines how an item or process could fail, what the effects would be, and which failure modes warrant priority under the chosen criticality method.

Criticality may be assessed qualitatively or calculated quantitatively; the selected method and assumptions should be stated.
