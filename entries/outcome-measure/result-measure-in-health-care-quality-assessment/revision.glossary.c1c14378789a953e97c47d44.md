# Outcome measure

*Scope: result measure in health-care quality assessment*

In health-care quality assessment, an outcome measure tracks a result of care, such as mortality or readmission rates, for a defined group and period.

It describes a result rather than a step in the care process intended to influence that result.

A change in the measure alone does not establish which action caused it.
