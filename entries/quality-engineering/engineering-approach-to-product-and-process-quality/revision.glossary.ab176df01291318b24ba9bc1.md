# Quality engineering

*Scope: engineering approach to product and process quality*

Quality engineering applies engineering and analytical methods across design and production to help products and processes meet intended quality requirements.

It can address variation and potential defects before delivery, as well as investigate and improve observed performance.

Its methods depend on the product and setting; software quality engineering, for example, places particular emphasis on requirements, testing, and release.
