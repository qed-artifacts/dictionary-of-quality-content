# Provenance

*Scope: traceable origin and history of an item*

Provenance is the traceable account of an item’s origin and history, including the people or systems and activities that created, changed, or transferred it.

For evidence, a provenance record can show which version was examined, where it came from, and how it passed through later hands or processes.

Good provenance makes an item easier to authenticate and interpret. It does not, on its own, establish that every claim made from the item is true.
