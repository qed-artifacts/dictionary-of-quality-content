# Intended use

*Scope: stated users, purpose and conditions for a designed product*

An intended use is a use for which a product is designed or represented as suitable, with its users, purpose, and conditions stated.

It gives design and validation a defined question: can the product serve those users under those conditions?

A foreseeable misuse may still call for risk assessment, but its foreseeability alone does not make it an intended use.
