# Trade-off

*Scope: choice among competing design outcomes*

A trade-off is a choice in which improving one valued outcome involves giving up some attainable level of another under specified constraints.

In a House of Quality, a negative interaction between proposed characteristics can signal a possible trade-off. The mark is a reason to investigate the design choices, not proof that no better combination is possible.

A defensible trade-off states the alternatives, the outcomes affected, and the reasons for accepting one balance over another. A compromise is justified by its context, not by a universal matrix score.
