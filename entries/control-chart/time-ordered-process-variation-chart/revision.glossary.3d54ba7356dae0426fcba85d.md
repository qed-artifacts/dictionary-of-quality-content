# Control chart

*Scope: time-ordered process variation chart*

A control chart is a time-ordered display of a process measure with a centerline and statistically derived control limits, used to detect signals of process change.

Points beyond a limit or certain nonrandom patterns can prompt investigation of a special cause. Chart type, sampling plan, and signal rules must fit the data and process.

Control limits describe expected process behavior under the chart model. They are different from specification limits, which state what an output is required to meet.
