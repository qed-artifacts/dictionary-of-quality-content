# Competence

*Scope: ability to perform a specified role or task*

Competence is the ability to perform a specified role or task using relevant knowledge and skills under the conditions that matter.

Training or a credential may provide evidence of competence, but neither alone shows that a person can perform the work.

A useful competence judgment identifies the task, expected level of performance, and conditions of performance.
