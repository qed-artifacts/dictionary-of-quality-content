# Supplier evaluation

*Scope: assessment of supplier capability and performance for a purchasing relationship*

Supplier evaluation is an assessment of whether a supplier can meet, and is meeting, requirements relevant to a purchasing relationship.

Before selection or during a contract, it may examine capability, product quality, delivery, compliance, and performance trends through records, surveys, or audits.

Its criteria depend on the product and its risks. Unlike incoming inspection, it assesses the supplier across a relationship rather than only a received lot.
