# Assessment of student learning

*Scope: evidence about student learning relative to expectations*

Assessment of student learning is the deliberate collection and interpretation of evidence about what students know or can do in relation to stated learning expectations.

Formative assessment uses findings to guide learning while it is underway; summative assessment appraises achievement at a defined point.

The evidence may come from work at the assignment, course, program, or degree level.
