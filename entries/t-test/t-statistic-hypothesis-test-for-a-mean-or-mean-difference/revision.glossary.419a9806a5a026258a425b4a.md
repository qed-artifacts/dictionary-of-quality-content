# t-test

*Scope: t-statistic hypothesis test for a mean or mean difference*

A t-test is a hypothesis test that uses a t statistic to assess a claim about a population mean or a difference between means.

One-sample and paired forms answer different questions, so the comparison and structure of the data must be specified.

Its result is interpreted under the test's assumptions and chosen significance level; statistical significance alone does not show that a difference is practically important.
