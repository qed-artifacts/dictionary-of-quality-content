# Information quality

*Scope: fitness of information for a specified use*

Information quality is how well information serves a specified purpose, given what it represents, how it was produced, and its relevant limitations.

Assessment may consider accuracy, completeness, timeliness, utility, integrity, and whether the supporting data and analysis fit the question. The choice of criteria depends on the use.

Clear presentation cannot make unsupported information reliable. Identify the source, methods, and uncertainty before using the information to justify a consequential claim.
