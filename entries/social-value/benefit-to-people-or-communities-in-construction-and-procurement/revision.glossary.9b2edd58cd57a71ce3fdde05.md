# Social value

*Scope: benefit to people or communities in construction and procurement*

In construction and public procurement, social value is a benefit to people or communities arising from a project or purchasing decision beyond its immediate deliverable.

Its content depends on whose benefit counts and the setting; employment and training opportunities may be relevant.

A project can seek social value before a benefit occurs, so stated aims and realized outcomes should be distinguished.
