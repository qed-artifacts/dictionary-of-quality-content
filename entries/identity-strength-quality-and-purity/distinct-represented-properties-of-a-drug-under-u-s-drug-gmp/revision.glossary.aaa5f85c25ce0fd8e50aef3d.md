# Identity, strength, quality, and purity

*Scope: distinct represented properties of a drug under U.S. drug GMP*

In U.S. drug manufacturing requirements, identity, strength, quality, and purity name distinct properties that a drug must possess as represented.

Identity concerns what the drug is; strength concerns its concentration or potency.

The four terms form a regulatory grouping, not four interchangeable names for quality; each is judged against applicable requirements and evidence.
