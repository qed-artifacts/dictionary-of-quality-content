# Cost

*Scope: organizational spending for an activity or option*

In organizational planning, a cost is an expenditure or resource commitment attributable to an activity, output, or proposed option.

Costs may be direct or indirect, and an estimate should state whose costs, which activities, and what period it covers.

An estimated cost differs from an expense already incurred; cost alone does not establish whether an option is worth pursuing.
