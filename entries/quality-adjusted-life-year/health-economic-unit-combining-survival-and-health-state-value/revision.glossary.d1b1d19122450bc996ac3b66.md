# Quality-adjusted life year

*Scope: health-economic unit combining survival and health-state value*

A quality-adjusted life year (QALY) is a unit that combines time lived with a weight for health-related quality of life during that time.

One year at a weight of 1 contributes one QALY; two years at a weight of 0.5 also contribute one.

The weights require a stated valuation method, and QALYs alone do not settle a treatment or resource-allocation decision.
