# Surveillance

*Scope: continuing or periodic assessment of a process in quality management*

In quality management, surveillance is continuing observation or periodic assessment of a process to see whether it still performs to a specified standard.

It looks for changes or emerging problems after a process has been put in use.

A surveillance check observes the process over time; it is not a one-time demonstration that every output conforms.
