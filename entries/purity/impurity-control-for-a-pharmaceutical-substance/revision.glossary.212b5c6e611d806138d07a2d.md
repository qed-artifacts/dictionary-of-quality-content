# Purity

*Scope: impurity control for a pharmaceutical substance*

For a pharmaceutical substance, purity is the extent to which the material is free of relevant impurities under specified tests.

Which impurities matter and what limits apply depend on the substance, its manufacturing process, and the intended product.

Purity is distinct from identity or potency: knowing what a substance is or how strongly it acts does not show what unwanted substances it contains.
