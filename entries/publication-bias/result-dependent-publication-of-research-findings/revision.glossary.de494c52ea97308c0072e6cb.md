# Publication bias

*Scope: result-dependent publication of research findings*

Publication bias occurs when whether research findings are published depends on the nature or direction of their results.

The available studies can then give a misleading picture of the evidence, for example if studies with favorable or statistically significant findings are more likely to appear.

It concerns missing studies or reports and is distinct from selectively reporting outcomes within a published study.
