# Sensory quality

*Scope: sensory characteristics of food*

In food assessment, sensory quality is how well a food's perceivable features meet relevant sensory expectations or criteria.

Appearance, aroma, flavor, and texture may matter; trained panels can describe or compare them under specified test conditions.

A favorable sensory judgment does not settle the food's safety or nutritional quality.
