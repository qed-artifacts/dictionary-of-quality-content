# Interaction

*Scope: factor-dependent effect in a designed experiment*

In a designed experiment, an interaction occurs when the effect of one factor on a response changes with the level of another factor.

For example, changing temperature might improve output at one pressure but worsen it at another. The result must therefore be interpreted for the relevant combination of factor levels.

A study that varies one factor at a time can miss this pattern. An interaction is a property of the studied response and setting, not a claim that the factors physically touch.
