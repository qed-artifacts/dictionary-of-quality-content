# Defects per million opportunities

*Scope: defect frequency normalized by defined error opportunities*

Defects per million opportunities is the number of observed defects divided by the total defined opportunities for a defect, then multiplied by one million.

When each inspected unit has the same number of opportunities, the denominator is units inspected times opportunities per unit.

Counting opportunities consistently matters: this rate counts defects, so one unit may contribute more than one.
