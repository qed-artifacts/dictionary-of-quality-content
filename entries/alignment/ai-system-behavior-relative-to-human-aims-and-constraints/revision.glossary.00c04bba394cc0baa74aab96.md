# Alignment

*Scope: AI-system behavior relative to human aims and constraints*

In AI, alignment concerns how well a system's behavior follows the human aims, instructions, and constraints specified for its use.

An AI system can perform a task capably yet still produce outputs that conflict with intended safety or preference requirements.

An alignment claim needs to identify whose aims matter, the use conditions, and the behavior used to test the claim.
