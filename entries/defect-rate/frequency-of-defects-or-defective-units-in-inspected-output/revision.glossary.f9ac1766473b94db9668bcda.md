# Defect rate

*Scope: frequency of defects or defective units in inspected output*

A defect rate expresses the frequency of defects or defective units relative to a stated amount of output.

State whether the numerator counts defects or units with at least one defect, and specify the denominator, such as inspected units; a result may be reported per million units.

The two counts can differ when one unit has several defects.
