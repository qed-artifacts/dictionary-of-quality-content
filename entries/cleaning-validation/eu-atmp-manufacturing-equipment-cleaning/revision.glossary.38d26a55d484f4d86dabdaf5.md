# Cleaning validation

*Scope: EU ATMP manufacturing equipment cleaning*

In advanced-therapy medicinal product manufacture, cleaning validation is documented evidence that a specified cleaning procedure repeatedly reduces relevant residues or contaminants below predefined acceptable limits.

The study defines the procedure, sampling approach, and acceptance criteria for the equipment and contamination risks concerned.

It assesses the procedure's repeatable capability; cleaning verification checks a particular batch or campaign after cleaning.
