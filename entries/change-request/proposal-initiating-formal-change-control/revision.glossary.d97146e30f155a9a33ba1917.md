# Change request

*Scope: proposal initiating formal change control*

In change control, a change request is a documented proposal to alter a controlled product, process, document, or system, submitted for evaluation before implementation.

It identifies the proposed change, why it is sought, and what may be affected. Reviewers then assess its effects and decide whether to authorize it.

Submitting a request starts the review; it does not itself permit the change to be made.
