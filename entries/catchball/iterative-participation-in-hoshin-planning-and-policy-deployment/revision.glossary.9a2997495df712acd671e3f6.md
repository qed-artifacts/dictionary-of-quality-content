# Catchball

*Scope: iterative participation in hoshin planning and policy deployment*

In hoshin planning, catchball is an iterative exchange of proposed goals and plans among organizational levels and functions to reach workable agreement.

People expected to carry out a plan can return concerns about resources, timing, or feasibility before the plan is settled.

The proposals move across and back through the organization as conflicts are resolved and action plans are refined.
