# Internal quality audit

*Scope: first-party audit of an organization's quality-related work*

An internal quality audit is an audit conducted by or on behalf of an organization to assess its own quality-related work against stated criteria.

It may examine a product or service, a process, or the quality management system, using collected evidence to identify conformity and gaps.

Auditors should be independent of the activity examined where practicable, even though the audit is internal.
