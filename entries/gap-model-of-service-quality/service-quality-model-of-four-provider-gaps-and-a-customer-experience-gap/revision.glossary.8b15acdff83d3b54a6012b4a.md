# Gap model of service quality

*Scope: service-quality model of four provider gaps and a customer experience gap*

The gap model of service quality explains differences between customers' expectations and perceived service through gaps in what a provider understands, specifies, delivers, and communicates.

The expectations-versus-perceived-performance difference is the final gap; four provider-side gaps are proposed contributors.

The framework helps locate a possible service problem, but a particular gap must be investigated rather than assumed from a low rating alone.
