# Bioavailability

*Scope: availability of an active drug after administration*

For a drug product, bioavailability describes how quickly and to what extent its active ingredient or active moiety becomes available at the site where it acts.

A product's stated drug content alone does not establish its bioavailability; formulation and administration can affect absorption.

Bioavailability may be a desired product characteristic, while dissolution is one laboratory observation that can help assess it for some dosage forms.
