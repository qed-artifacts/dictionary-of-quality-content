# Patient-centeredness

*Scope: individualized health-care relationship and decisions*

In health care, patient-centeredness is the extent to which care responds to a patient's individual circumstances, experience, needs, and preferences.

It includes understanding how the person experiences illness, sharing relevant information, and involving the person in decisions about care.

Its components can vary by setting, so a single behavior does not by itself establish that care is patient-centered.
