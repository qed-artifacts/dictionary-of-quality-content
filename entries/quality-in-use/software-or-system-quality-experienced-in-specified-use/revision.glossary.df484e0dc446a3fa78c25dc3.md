# Quality in use

*Scope: software or system quality experienced in specified use*

In software and systems evaluation, quality in use is how well specified users achieve their goals with a product or system in a specified context of use.

Common assessment dimensions include effectiveness, efficiency, satisfaction, freedom from risk, and coverage of intended contexts. Assessing these requires attention to users, tasks, and conditions of use.

A product can meet a technical specification yet perform poorly in use. A claim of quality in use therefore needs evidence from the relevant context, not only a list of product features.
