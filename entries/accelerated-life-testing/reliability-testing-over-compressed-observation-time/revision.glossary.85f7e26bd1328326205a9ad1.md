# Accelerated life testing

*Scope: reliability testing over compressed observation time*

Accelerated life testing subjects items to more intensive use or more severe conditions to learn about their failure or lifetime behavior sooner than ordinary use would allow.

Continuous use can compress elapsed test time without increasing the operating stress; elevated stress is a different route to acceleration.

When elevated-stress results are used to estimate normal-life behavior, the stress relationship and failure mechanisms need justification.
