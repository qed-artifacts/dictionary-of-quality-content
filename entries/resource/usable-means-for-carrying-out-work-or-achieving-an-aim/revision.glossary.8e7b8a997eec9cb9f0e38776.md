# Resource

*Scope: usable means for carrying out work or achieving an aim*

A resource is something available for use in carrying out an activity or pursuing an aim.

Time, people, skills, money, equipment, materials, space, and information can all serve as resources, depending on the task.

Some resources can be reused, while others are consumed or depleted; identifying a resource does not establish that enough of it is available.
