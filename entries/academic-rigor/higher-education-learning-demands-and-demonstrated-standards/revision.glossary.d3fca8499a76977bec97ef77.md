# Academic rigor

*Scope: higher-education learning demands and demonstrated standards*

In higher education, academic rigor is the appropriately challenging standard of learning and demonstrated performance expected in a course or program.

It appears in the learning outcomes, tasks, and criteria used to judge what students can do.

A course can support students through difficult work while keeping its academic standard clear; difficulty alone does not establish rigor.
