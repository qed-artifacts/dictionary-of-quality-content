# Causal factor

*Scope: potential or established contributor to a specified effect in quality analysis*

A causal factor is a condition or action that contributes to a specified outcome or problem.

Name the outcome and the way the factor is thought to affect it; a cause-and-effect diagram can organize candidate factors.

Listing a factor on a diagram proposes a causal explanation, which still needs evidence before it is treated as established.
