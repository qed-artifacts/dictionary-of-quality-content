# Service reliability

*Scope: dependable delivery of a promised service*

In service-quality assessment, service reliability is the extent to which a provider delivers a promised service dependably and accurately across relevant occasions.

A booking that is honored once is one result; reliability concerns whether the provider can be counted on to honor comparable bookings.

The promise, conditions, and occasions of service must be clear before reliability can be assessed.
