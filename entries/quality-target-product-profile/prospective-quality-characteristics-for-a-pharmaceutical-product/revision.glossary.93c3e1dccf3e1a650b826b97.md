# Quality target product profile

*Scope: prospective quality characteristics for a pharmaceutical product*

In pharmaceutical development, a quality target product profile is a prospective outline of the characteristics a drug product should ideally have to achieve its intended quality, taking safety and efficacy into account.

It helps guide development choices and the identification of potential critical quality attributes.

The profile states a target for development, not proof that a finished product already meets it.
