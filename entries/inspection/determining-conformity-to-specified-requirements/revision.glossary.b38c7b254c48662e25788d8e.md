# Inspection

*Scope: determining conformity to specified requirements*

Inspection is an examination used to determine whether an object meets specified requirements.

The object, requirements, and inspection method determine what the result establishes. A passing sample does not by itself establish that every item or future output will conform.

Inspection can supply evidence for quality control or assurance. It does not by itself settle whether the requirements are suitable for the object’s intended use.
