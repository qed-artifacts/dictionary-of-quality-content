# Healthcare quality

*Scope: quality of care and its delivery*

Healthcare quality is how well care and its delivery meet justified health needs while avoiding preventable harm.

Safety, effectiveness, responsiveness to patients, timeliness, efficiency, and equity are distinct aspects of a judgment about care. A favorable result in one aspect does not settle the others.

State the patient group, setting, and purpose of care before judging it. An outcome alone may not show whether care was safe, timely, or fair.
