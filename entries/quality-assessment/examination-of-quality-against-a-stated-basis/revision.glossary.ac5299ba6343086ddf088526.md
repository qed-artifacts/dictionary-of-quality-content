# Quality assessment

*Scope: examination of quality against a stated basis*

Quality assessment is the examination of evidence about an object’s quality against relevant requirements or criteria.

It may examine characteristics, performance, processes, or outcomes. State what was examined, the basis of comparison, and the limits of the evidence.

An assessment can report findings that support a later quality judgment, or include that judgment itself. The precise boundary between quality assessment and quality evaluation depends on the field and task.
