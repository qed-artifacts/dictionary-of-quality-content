# Quality standard

*Scope: documented quality reference within a stated scope*

A quality standard is a documented reference for specifying, managing, or evaluating quality within a stated scope.

Its provisions may set requirements or provide a model or guidance. Identify the document, edition, subject, and which provisions apply before claiming that something conforms to it.

Conformity to a quality-management reference does not by itself show that every product or service is good for its intended users. A separate judgment must connect the applicable requirements to the object and evidence.
