# Management review

*Scope: leadership review of a quality management system*

Management review is a planned examination by responsible leaders of whether a quality management system remains suitable and effective for its objectives.

Relevant inputs can include performance trends, complaints, audits, changes, and follow-up from earlier reviews. The point is to interpret those inputs and make decisions, not merely receive a report.

The resulting actions may change processes, resources, policy, or objectives. Record who is responsible and later check whether the actions had the intended effect.
