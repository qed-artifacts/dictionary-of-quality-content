# Research quality

*Scope: merit of inquiry and its outputs for a stated purpose*

Research quality is how well an inquiry and its outputs meet justified standards for their purpose and field.

Assessment may consider rigor, originality, significance, and whether the evidence warrants the conclusions; their weight depends on the research question and context.

A journal label, citation count, or attractive result does not by itself settle the quality of a particular study.
