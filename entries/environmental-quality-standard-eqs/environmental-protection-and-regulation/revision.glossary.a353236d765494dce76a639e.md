# Environmental quality standard (EQS)

*Scope: environmental protection and regulation*

An environmental quality standard (EQS) is a limit for the concentration of a specified substance in an environment, set to protect people or ecosystems from harm.

It concerns the receiving environment, such as a body of water, rather than the amount released from a particular source. Meeting one EQS does not establish that the environment is safe in every respect.
