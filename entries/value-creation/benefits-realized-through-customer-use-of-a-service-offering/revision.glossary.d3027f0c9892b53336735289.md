# Value creation

*Scope: benefits realized through customer use of a service offering*

In a value-in-use account of services, value creation is the emergence of benefits as a customer uses an offering together with their own activities and resources.

A provider can enable that use, but the offering alone does not guarantee the benefit.

What is valuable, and how much, can differ across customers and situations.
