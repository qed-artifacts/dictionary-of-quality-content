# Practical significance

*Scope: real-world importance of a statistical difference for a decision*

Practical significance is the importance of an observed difference for a real decision in its setting.

It depends on the size and consequences of the difference, not only on whether a statistical test rejects a null hypothesis.

With a large sample, a very small difference can be statistically significant while having little practical importance.
