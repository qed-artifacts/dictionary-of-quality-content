# Survey

*Scope: questionnaire-based collection of responses from a group*

In evaluation research, a survey is a planned collection of responses from a specified group using a set of questions.

A survey design states whom the questions are meant to represent, how respondents are selected, and how answers are collected.

Answers report respondents' views or experiences; they do not automatically prove how people behave.
