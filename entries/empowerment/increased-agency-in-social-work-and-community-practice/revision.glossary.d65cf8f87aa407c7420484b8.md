# Empowerment

*Scope: increased agency in social work and community practice*

In social work, empowerment is a process through which people or communities gain practical influence over decisions and conditions affecting their lives.

It builds on their own capacities and gives their voices a direct part in identifying problems and solutions.

Providing a service without changing the person's agency does not by itself establish empowerment.
