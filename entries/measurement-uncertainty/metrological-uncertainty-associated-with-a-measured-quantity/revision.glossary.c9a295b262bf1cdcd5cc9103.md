# Measurement uncertainty

*Scope: metrological uncertainty associated with a measured quantity*

Measurement uncertainty describes the dispersion of values reasonably attributable to a specified measurand, given the information used in measuring it.

It is not the same as measurement error, which compares a measured value with a reference value. An uncertainty assessment identifies relevant contributors and how they are combined.

When a result is used to decide conformity, the uncertainty and the decision rule both matter. A reported interval or coverage factor must be interpreted under its stated method and conditions.
