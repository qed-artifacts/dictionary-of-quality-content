# Root cause

*Scope: cause of a nonconformance targeted for corrective action*

A root cause is a condition or mechanism that produced a defined problem and can be addressed to reduce its recurrence.

In quality improvement, identifying a root cause connects a nonconformance to a corrective action directed at why it occurred.

A proposed cause remains a hypothesis until evidence supports the causal link; a problem may have more than one contributing cause.
