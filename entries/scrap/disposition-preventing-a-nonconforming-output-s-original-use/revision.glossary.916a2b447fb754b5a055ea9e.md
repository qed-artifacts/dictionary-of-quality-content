# Scrap

*Scope: disposition preventing a nonconforming output's original use*

In quality management, to scrap a nonconforming product or service is to dispose of it so it cannot be used for its originally intended purpose.

Scrap is a disposition of the affected output, rather than work to make that output conform.

It prevents the original use; it does not by itself correct the cause of the nonconformity.
