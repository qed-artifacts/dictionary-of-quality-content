# Organizational leadership

*Scope: directing and enabling coordinated organizational action*

Organizational leadership is the activity of setting direction and enabling people and structures to work toward an organization's aims.

It connects a mission and strategy to decisions about responsibilities, authority, coordination, and support.

Leadership can be exercised by people without a formal management title; a title alone does not establish that someone is leading effectively.
