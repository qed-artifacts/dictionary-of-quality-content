# Accountability

*Scope: answerability for conduct or results to an evaluating party*

Accountability is a relationship in which a person or organization must give an account of its actions or results to another party able to assess it.

The relationship identifies who answers to whom, for what, and under which rules or possible consequences.

Having a responsibility is not the whole relationship: accountability also requires an account that another party can examine.
