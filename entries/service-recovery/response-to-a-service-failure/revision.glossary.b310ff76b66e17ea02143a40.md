# Service recovery

*Scope: response to a service failure*

Service recovery is the action a provider takes to address a service failure and its effects on the people receiving the service.

It may involve correcting the immediate problem, making amends, and learning why the failure occurred so it is less likely to recur.

Recovery can begin before a customer complains; handling a complaint is only one possible route into it.
