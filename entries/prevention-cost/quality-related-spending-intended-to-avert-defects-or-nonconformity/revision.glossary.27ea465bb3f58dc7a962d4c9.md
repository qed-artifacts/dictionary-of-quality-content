# Prevention cost

*Scope: quality-related spending intended to avert defects or nonconformity*

A prevention cost is a cost incurred for an activity designed to keep defects or other quality failures from occurring.

Examples include quality planning, training, and changes that reduce opportunities for error. The activity aims to prevent a problem before it arises.

In cost-of-quality accounting, prevention is distinct from appraisal, which checks outputs or processes, and from costs caused by failures. Classify a mixed activity by a stated accounting rule and avoid counting it twice.
