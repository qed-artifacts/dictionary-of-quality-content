# Certainty of evidence

*Scope: GRADE confidence in an outcome-specific evidence body*

In GRADE reviews, certainty of evidence is a judgment about how much confidence to place in the evidence for a specified outcome.

It is rated high, moderate, low, or very low after considering such concerns as bias, inconsistency, indirectness, imprecision, and publication bias.

The rating belongs to an outcome and its supporting evidence, not automatically to an entire study or review.
