# Quality level

*Scope: degree of service quality relative to stated expectations or standards*

In service management, a quality level is the degree of service quality achieved or sought relative to customer expectations and service standards.

Calling a level high or low requires stating the features being assessed and the basis for comparison.

An economically optimal quality level is a separate business judgment about costs and benefits, not a universal target.
