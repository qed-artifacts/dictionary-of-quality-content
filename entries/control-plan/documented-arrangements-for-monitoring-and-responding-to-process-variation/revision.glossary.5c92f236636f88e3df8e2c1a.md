# Control plan

*Scope: documented arrangements for monitoring and responding to process variation*

A control plan is a document specifying how a defined process and its important characteristics will be monitored and what action follows when results depart from stated conditions.

It commonly names what to check, the method and frequency, the acceptable limits, who records results, and what to do when a limit is breached.

A chart or target alone is not a complete control plan: people also need responsibility and response instructions for ongoing operation.
