# Mean time to repair

*Scope: average corrective-maintenance duration for repairable equipment*

For a repairable system, mean time to repair (MTTR) is the average counted downtime needed to restore it after a failure.

For a set of failures, divide the counted repair downtime by the number of failures; state which delays the downtime includes.

MTTR concerns restoration time, whereas mean time between failures concerns operating time between failures. Neither figure alone fully determines availability.
