# Commissioning

*Scope: testing building systems before operational use*

In construction, commissioning is a planned process for checking that installed building systems work as intended for the owner's requirements.

It can include project-specific functional tests, documentation, and training for people who will operate or maintain the systems.

Commissioning supplements ordinary start-up and balancing tests; completing installation alone does not establish that the systems perform as required.
