# Assurance

*Scope: systems-engineering support for a claim*

In systems engineering, assurance is a reasoned basis for trusting a particular claim about a system's behavior or properties.

Name the claim and the grounds supporting it. The justification needed depends on the consequences if the system fails to behave as claimed.

Claims can concern safety, reliability, resilience, security, or privacy; assurance supports confidence without guaranteeing the outcome.
