# Pollution

*Scope: environmental contamination harmful to living communities*

In environmental assessment, pollution is the presence or buildup of material in a place at a level that can harm organisms or ecosystems.

A detectable contaminant is not automatically a pollutant; concentration, exposure, and potential effects matter.

Assessment may involve air, water, soil, and the organisms that take up contaminants.
