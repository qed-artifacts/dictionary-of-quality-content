# Acceptance quality limit

*Scope: sampling-inspection limit for a satisfactory process average over a series of lots*

In acceptance sampling over a continuing series of lots, the acceptance quality limit (AQL) is a designated quality level at the boundary of what is treated as a satisfactory process average for sampling inspection.

It helps specify a sampling plan's intended performance for a continuing process.

It is not a promise that every accepted lot meets that level or contains no nonconforming units.
