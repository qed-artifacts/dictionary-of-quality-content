# Tangibles

*Scope: visible physical features in service-quality assessment*

In service-quality assessment, tangibles are the visible physical features of a service setting, its equipment, and the appearance of personnel.

A clean waiting area or well-kept equipment can shape a customer's impression of a service.

Those cues do not by themselves establish that the service is reliable, accurate, or responsive.
