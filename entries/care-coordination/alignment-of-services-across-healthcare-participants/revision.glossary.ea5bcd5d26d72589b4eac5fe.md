# Care coordination

*Scope: alignment of services across healthcare participants*

Care coordination is the deliberate alignment of care activities among the people and services involved in a patient's care.

It includes sharing relevant information and arranging how one service connects with the next, especially when the patient moves between settings.

Its aim is that the parts of care work together for the patient rather than proceed as disconnected episodes.
