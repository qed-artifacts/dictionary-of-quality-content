# Timeliness

*Scope: suitability of delivery or availability time for an intended use*

Timeliness is how well an action, service, or information occurs or becomes available when it is needed for its intended use.

A delay matters when it impairs the use or outcome at issue; a fast response is not automatically timely if it arrives at the wrong moment.

The relevant time window depends on the task and recipient, as in urgent care or a report needed for a decision.
