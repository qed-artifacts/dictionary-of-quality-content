# LTPD

*Scope: lot tolerance percent defective in acceptance sampling*

Lot tolerance percent defective (LTPD) is a specified percentage of defective units at which an acceptance sampling plan has a low probability of accepting a lot.

It marks a poor-lot quality point on the plan's operating-characteristic curve; the associated probability of acceptance expresses the consumer's risk at that point.

LTPD is a property of the sampling plan, not a measurement of the defective percentage in a particular lot.
