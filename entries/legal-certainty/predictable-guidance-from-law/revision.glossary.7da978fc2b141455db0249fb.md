# Legal certainty

*Scope: predictable guidance from law*

In rule-of-law discussions, legal certainty is the degree to which people can identify the rules governing their conduct and reasonably anticipate how those rules will be applied.

Clear, publicly accessible rules and regular procedures help people rely on the law for guidance.

Legal certainty does not promise that every case has an unambiguous outcome.
