# Environmental quality

*Scope: conditions of an environment relative to ecological or human purposes*

Environmental quality is how favorable the conditions of a specified environment are for relevant ecological or human purposes.

A judgment identifies the place, time, affected living systems or people, and criteria. Air, water, and soil conditions can each matter, but no single indicator describes the whole environment.

An acceptable limit for one pollutant answers a narrower question than whether the environment is good overall. The assessment also depends on the chosen reference condition, exposure, and intended use.
