# Consumer surplus

*Scope: buyer benefit expressed as willingness-to-pay gap*

Consumer surplus is the difference between the most a buyer would pay for a good or service and the amount actually paid.

For a particular purchase, it expresses the buyer's modeled gain from paying less than that maximum.

Its estimate depends on how willingness to pay is assessed; it is neither the seller's profit nor a complete account of the good's value.
