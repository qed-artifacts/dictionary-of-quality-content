# Quality trilogy

*Scope: Juran's quality-management model*

The quality trilogy is Juran's model of three linked management processes: quality planning, quality control, and quality improvement.

Planning prepares a process to meet quality goals, control manages performance during operation, and improvement reduces persistent shortcomings.

The model treats improvement as additional work beyond keeping an existing process within its current operating pattern.
