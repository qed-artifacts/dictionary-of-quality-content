# Quality plan

*Scope: quality planning for a specified product, project, or contract*

A quality plan states how quality requirements will be addressed for a specified product, project, or contract.

It identifies the relevant activities and controls and, where needed, who will carry them out and what records will show the result.

It may be a separate document or part of a broader project plan, depending on the work.
