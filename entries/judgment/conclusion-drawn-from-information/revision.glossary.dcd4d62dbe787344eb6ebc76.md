# Judgment

*Scope: conclusion drawn from information*

A judgment is a conclusion reached about a matter by interpreting information in light of a relevant basis for deciding.

The basis may be a rule, criterion, comparison, or informed professional appraisal. A useful judgment makes that basis and any important uncertainty visible.

Judgments may be factual or evaluative. A quality judgment is the narrower case that appraises how well an object meets relevant quality requirements or criteria.
