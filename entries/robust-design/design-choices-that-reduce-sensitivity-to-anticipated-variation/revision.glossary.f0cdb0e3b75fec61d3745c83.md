# Robust design

*Scope: design choices that reduce sensitivity to anticipated variation*

Robust design is an approach to choosing product or process characteristics so performance changes less when anticipated sources of variation change.

A design may be tested across relevant materials, manufacturing conditions, environments, or patterns of use. The aim is to keep important performance characteristics near their intended values despite such “noise.”

Robustness is relative to the variation considered. A robust design is not immune to every condition, and performance outside the tested range needs separate evidence.
