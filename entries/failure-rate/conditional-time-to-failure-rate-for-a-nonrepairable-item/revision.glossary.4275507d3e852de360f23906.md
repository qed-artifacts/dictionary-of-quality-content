# Failure rate

*Scope: conditional time-to-failure rate for a nonrepairable item*

In time-to-failure reliability analysis, the failure rate at a given time describes how rapidly items still functioning then fail.

It relates failures during a short interval to the items that had survived to its start and to the length of that interval.

The rate can change with age and operating conditions; it should not be treated as a universal constant or confused with the fraction that has failed since the start.
