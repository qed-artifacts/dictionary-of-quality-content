# Process analytical technology

*Scope: pharmaceutical manufacturing measurement and control*

Process analytical technology (PAT) is an approach to designing, monitoring, and controlling pharmaceutical manufacturing through timely measurement of material and process attributes relevant to product quality.

Measurements taken during processing can help reveal variation early enough to guide control of the process.

PAT is broader than a single sensor or test: it joins measurement with process understanding and control.
