# Testing

*Scope: product or component assessment in quality engineering*

In quality engineering, testing applies defined conditions or procedures to an item to obtain evidence about its characteristics or capabilities.

A test may expose the item to physical, chemical, environmental, or operating conditions. It may be destructive or nondestructive.

The observed or measured result needs interpretation against a relevant requirement before a conformity judgment is made. Testing and inspection overlap, but they are not interchangeable in every use.
