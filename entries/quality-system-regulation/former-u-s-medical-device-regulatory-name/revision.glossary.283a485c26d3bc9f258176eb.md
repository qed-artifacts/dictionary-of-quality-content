# Quality System Regulation

*Scope: former U.S. medical-device regulatory name*

Quality System Regulation (QSR) was the former U.S. FDA name for the medical-device quality-system requirements in 21 CFR Part 820.

Since February 2, 2026, the revised Part 820 is called the Quality Management System Regulation (QMSR). The older name remains useful for reading historical guidance and records.
