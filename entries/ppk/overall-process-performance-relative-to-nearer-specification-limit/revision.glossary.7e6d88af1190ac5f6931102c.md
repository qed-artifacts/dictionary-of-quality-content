# Ppk

*Scope: overall process performance relative to nearer specification limit*

Ppk is a process performance index relating the process average's distance from the nearer specification limit to the overall variation in observed output.

Unlike Pp, it reflects how far the process is off center as well as its spread.

Its interpretation still depends on the process's stability, output distribution, and specified limits.
