# Average outgoing quality

*Scope: expected outgoing nonconforming fraction under rectifying sampling*

Average outgoing quality (AOQ) is the expected fraction of nonconforming units leaving a rectifying acceptance-sampling process at a stated incoming quality level.

Accepted lots pass through, while rejected lots are inspected and nonconforming units are replaced under the assumed plan.

AOQ describes a long-run expectation for that plan and incoming quality; it is not the measured quality of one outgoing lot.
