# Reference standard

*Scope: measurement standard used to calibrate other local standards*

In metrology, a reference standard is a measurement standard designated by an organization or at a location for calibrating other standards of the same kind of quantity.

It serves as a reference in that calibration chain; the designation does not by itself make it a primary or international standard.

Its use should be identified by the quantity and the standards it is intended to calibrate.
