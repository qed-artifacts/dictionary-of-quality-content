# Time horizon

*Scope: period considered in a decision or evaluation*

A time horizon is the span of time over which a decision, forecast, or evaluation considers outcomes.

State when the period begins and ends, or how long it lasts. A short horizon may reveal immediate costs and benefits while missing effects that emerge later.

The time horizon is part of the question being asked. It is not the same as a deadline for making the decision or finishing the work.
