# Quality culture

*Scope: shared organizational expectations and practice*

Quality culture is the shared expectations and everyday practices through which an organization pursues, examines, and improves quality.

It becomes visible in what leaders and staff do: how concerns are communicated, how performance is examined, and whether learning changes routine work.

A stated value or a formal quality procedure is not sufficient evidence of such a culture on its own. Look for consistent behavior across roles and over time.
