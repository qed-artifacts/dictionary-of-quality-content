# Quality appraisal

*Scope: methodological appraisal of studies in evidence synthesis*

In evidence synthesis, quality appraisal is a structured assessment of the methodological strengths and limitations of the studies used to support a finding.

The criteria should fit the study methods, and the appraisal helps interpret how much confidence to place in the synthesized finding.

A polished report alone does not show that the underlying study methods were sound.
