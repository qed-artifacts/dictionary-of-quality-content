# Quality claim

*Scope: assertion about an object's quality*

A quality claim is an assertion about how well a specified object satisfies a relevant quality requirement or criterion.

A useful claim names the object, the aspect being judged, the basis for judgment, and the evidence that supports it.

A measured value alone is not a complete quality claim until it is related to a requirement or evaluative criterion.
