# Perceptual quality

*Scope: listener's appraisal of synthesized speech*

For synthesized speech, perceptual quality is a listener's appraisal of how well the speech they hear matches relevant expectations or requirements.

Listeners can attend to features such as naturalness and intelligibility as well as overall sound.

An instrumental score can help assess the signal, but it does not by itself express every aspect of the listening experience.
