# Risk tolerance

*Scope: readiness to bear specified residual risk*

In organizational risk management, risk tolerance is the level of a specified risk that an organization or stakeholder is prepared to bear while pursuing an objective.

It can be assessed after available risk treatments and may differ among the people who own different risks.

It is a more particular acceptance judgment than an organization's broad risk appetite; it need not be a single numerical maximum.
