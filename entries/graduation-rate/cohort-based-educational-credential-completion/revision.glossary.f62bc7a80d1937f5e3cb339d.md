# Graduation rate

*Scope: cohort-based educational credential completion*

A graduation rate is the proportion of a defined entering student cohort that earns a specified credential within a specified time.

The cohort, permitted exclusions, credential, and completion window must be stated to interpret the figure.

Changing the denominator or time window can change the rate even when the same students' records are used.
