# 5S

*Scope: Lean workplace organization and maintenance method*

5S is a Lean method for organizing and maintaining a workplace through five practices: sort, set in order, shine, standardize, and sustain.

The practices remove unneeded items, locate needed ones, keep the area clean, make arrangements consistent, and maintain them over time.

A one-time cleanup is incomplete without standards and sustained use.
