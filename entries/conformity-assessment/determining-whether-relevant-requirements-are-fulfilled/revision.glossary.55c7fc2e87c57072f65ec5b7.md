# Conformity assessment

*Scope: determining whether relevant requirements are fulfilled*

Conformity assessment is the determination of whether an object fulfills relevant specified requirements.

The object, requirements, assessment method, and scope must be clear. Depending on the case, assessment may use testing, inspection, or certification.

A conformity result addresses the requirements actually assessed; it does not, by itself, establish overall excellence or fitness for every use.
