# Public

*Scope: people concerned by a public decision*

In public decision making, the public is the people and communities who may be affected by a decision or have a legitimate interest in it.

The relevant public depends on the decision and may include people who are absent from formal consultation.

The word does not mean that everyone shares one viewpoint, and this sense differs from calling a record publicly accessible.
