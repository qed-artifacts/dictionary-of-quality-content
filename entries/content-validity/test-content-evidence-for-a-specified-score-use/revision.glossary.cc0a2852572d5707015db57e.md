# Content validity

*Scope: test-content evidence for a specified score use*

Content validity concerns whether a test's items and tasks adequately represent the domain it is meant to assess for a specified interpretation and use.

Important parts of the domain should be covered without letting irrelevant material dominate.

Reviewing content supplies one kind of validity evidence; it does not establish that every proposed use of the scores is warranted.
