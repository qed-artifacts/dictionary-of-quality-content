# Generalizability

*Scope: warranted extension of research findings*

In research, generalizability is the extent to which findings from studied cases can reasonably be applied to other people, times, or settings.

The intended group or setting beyond the study must be identified before judging whether the extension is warranted.

A study may answer its own question well without aiming to generalize beyond its particular cases.
