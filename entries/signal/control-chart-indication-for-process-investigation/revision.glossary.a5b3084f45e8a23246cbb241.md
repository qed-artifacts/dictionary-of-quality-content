# Signal

*Scope: control-chart indication for process investigation*

In statistical process control, a signal is a chart result or pattern that meets a stated rule for investigating a possible change in the process.

A point beyond a control limit is one example; patterns among plotted points can also be assessed under the chart’s rules.

A signal calls for investigation. It does not prove a particular cause, and even a stable process can occasionally produce a false alarm.
