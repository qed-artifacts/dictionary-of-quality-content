# Governance quality

*Scope: evaluation of public governing arrangements and conduct*

In public administration, governance quality is how well governing arrangements and decisions meet relevant standards for exercising public authority.

The appraisal may consider accountability, transparency, effectiveness, fairness, and the rule of law, with their relevance made explicit for the case.

Efficient service delivery alone cannot settle the judgment if other governing standards are also at stake.
