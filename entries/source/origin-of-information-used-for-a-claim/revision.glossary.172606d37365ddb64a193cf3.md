# Source

*Scope: origin of information used for a claim*

In an inquiry, a source is a person, record, dataset, or other origin from which information relevant to a claim is obtained.

Identify the particular source and the version or location used so someone can inspect the same material. Testimony, documents, and other records can play different evidential roles.

A citation identifies where information came from; it does not by itself show that the source is accurate or that it supports the claim made from it.
