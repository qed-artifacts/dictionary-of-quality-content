# Organizational learning

*Scope: collective sharing, testing, and retention of work knowledge*

Organizational learning is the development and retention of shared knowledge about an organization's work through experience, inquiry, and feedback.

People interpret what happened, share what they learned, and test possible changes to their practices.

A lesson held only in one person's memory may be useful to that person, but the organization has limited means to retain or use it across future work.
