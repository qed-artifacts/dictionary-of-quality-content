# Consequence

*Scope: effect of an event or action on an objective or interest*

A consequence is an effect that follows from an event, action, or decision and matters to an objective or interest.

A decision may have several consequences, favorable or harmful, immediate or delayed. Specify the effect, who or what bears it, and the period considered.

An outcome after a decision is not automatically caused by that decision. Assess alternative causes and uncertainty before assigning responsibility for the consequence.
