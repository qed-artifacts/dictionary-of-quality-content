# Data validity

*Scope: data-quality conformity to specified definitions and rules*

In data-quality management, data validity is the extent to which values or records conform to applicable data definitions and rules.

A rule may specify an allowed format, range, or relationship between fields.

A validly formatted record can still misstate the world; validity in this sense does not by itself establish factual accuracy.
