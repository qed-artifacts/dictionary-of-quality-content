# Cost-benefit analysis

*Scope: monetized appraisal of local public project alternatives*

For a local public project, cost-benefit analysis compares its expected benefits and costs with those of relevant alternatives, expressing them in money terms to estimate net benefit.

It identifies whose benefits and costs count and can discount future amounts so they can be compared on a common basis.

The calculation cannot by itself settle questions about rights or distribution, even when it favors a proposal.
