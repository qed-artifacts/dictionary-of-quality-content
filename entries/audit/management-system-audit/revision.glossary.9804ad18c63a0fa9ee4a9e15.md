# Audit

*Scope: management-system audit*

In management-system work, an audit is a systematic, independent, and documented examination of evidence against stated criteria to determine how far those criteria are met.

The audit's scope identifies the activities, locations, and period covered.

A finding depends on the evidence and criteria examined; an audit is not a guarantee that every instance or future result will conform.
