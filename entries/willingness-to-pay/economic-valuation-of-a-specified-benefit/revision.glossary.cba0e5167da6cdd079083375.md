# Willingness to pay

*Scope: economic valuation of a specified benefit*

Willingness to pay is the most money or purchasing power a person would give up to obtain a specified benefit under stated conditions.

It expresses a tradeoff against other possible uses of that person's resources.

The stated amount depends on the benefit, circumstances, and resources available; it is not a context-free measure of the benefit's worth.
