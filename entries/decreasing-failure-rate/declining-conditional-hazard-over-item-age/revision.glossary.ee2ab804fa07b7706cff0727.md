# Decreasing failure rate

*Scope: declining conditional hazard over item age*

A decreasing failure rate means that, over a specified period, the instantaneous rate of failure falls as the surviving items age.

It can describe an early-life period in which vulnerable items fail and the remaining population has a lower conditional failure rate.

The pattern concerns the population of survivors; it does not mean that every individual item becomes more reliable as it ages.
