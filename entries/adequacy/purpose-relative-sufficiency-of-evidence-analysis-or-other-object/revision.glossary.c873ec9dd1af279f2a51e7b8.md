# Adequacy

*Scope: purpose-relative sufficiency of evidence, analysis or other object*

Adequacy is being sufficient for a specified purpose or requirement.

An analysis can be adequate for one question yet inadequate for another because the data and method must fit the goal.

Adequacy marks a defensible sufficiency judgment; it does not by itself mean the work is excellent or free of limitations.
