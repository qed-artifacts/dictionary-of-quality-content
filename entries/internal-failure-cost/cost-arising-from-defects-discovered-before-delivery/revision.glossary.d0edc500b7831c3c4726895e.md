# Internal failure cost

*Scope: cost arising from defects discovered before delivery*

An internal failure cost is a cost arising from a defect or nonconformity found before a product or service is delivered.

Scrap, rework, and review of defective material are typical examples. The failure is discovered within the producing organization’s delivery boundary.

Internal and external failure costs differ by when the problem is found relative to delivery, not by whether the failure is serious. State the boundary for a multistage service or supply chain.
