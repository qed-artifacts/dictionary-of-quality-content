# Confidence interval

*Scope: frequentist interval estimation of a population parameter*

A confidence interval is an interval calculated from sample data by a method designed to capture a specified population parameter at a stated rate across repeated samples.

For example, under the method's assumptions, a 95% confidence-interval procedure covers the fixed parameter in about 95% of repeated applications.

The stated percentage describes the procedure's long-run coverage, not the probability that the fixed parameter lies in one already calculated interval.
