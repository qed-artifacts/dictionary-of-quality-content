# Cost of poor quality (COPQ)

*Scope: costs attributable to defects and other quality failures*

Cost of poor quality (COPQ) is the estimated cost attributable to failures to meet relevant quality requirements.

It can include scrap and rework found before delivery, and returns, warranty work, complaints, or other consequences found afterward. Some costs are difficult to observe or assign.

An estimate needs a stated accounting boundary, time period, and basis for attributing costs. Prevention and appraisal costs are often reported separately as costs of achieving quality; naming the convention avoids misleading comparisons.
