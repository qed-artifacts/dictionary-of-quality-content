# Maintainability

*Scope: ability to maintain or restore an engineered item*

For an engineered item, maintainability is how readily it can be kept in or restored to a required condition through maintenance under stated conditions.

The assessment depends on the work, available procedures and resources, and the skills of those performing it.

It concerns carrying out maintenance, not how often the item fails.
