# Utility

*Scope: numerical representation of preferences in decision theory*

In decision theory, utility is a numerical representation of how a decision maker ranks outcomes or choices under a specified model of preferences.

A utility function assigns numbers so the model can compare alternatives, including uncertain ones when probabilities are also specified.

Utility numbers are model-dependent; they need not equal money or express a universal measure of human well-being.
