# Hazard analysis and critical control points (HACCP)

*Scope: preventive food-safety control system*

Hazard analysis and critical control points (HACCP) is a preventive food-safety system that identifies significant hazards and controls them at specified steps in a process.

A HACCP plan identifies critical control points, sets critical limits, monitors them, specifies corrective action, and records and verifies how the controls work. Prerequisite programs provide basic conditions for the plan.

A critical control point is a step where control can prevent, eliminate, or reduce a food-safety hazard to an acceptable level. HACCP does not mean that testing a finished product alone is sufficient to control the process.
