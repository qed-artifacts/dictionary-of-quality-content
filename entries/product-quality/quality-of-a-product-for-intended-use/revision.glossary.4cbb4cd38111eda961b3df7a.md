# Product quality

*Scope: quality of a product for intended use*

Product quality is how well a product’s relevant characteristics fulfill requirements for its intended use.

The requirements and characteristics depend on the product and setting: a medicine and a software system call for different evidence. State the users, conditions of use, and characteristics being judged.

Evidence about how a product was made can support the judgment, but it does not replace examination of the product and its performance against applicable requirements.
