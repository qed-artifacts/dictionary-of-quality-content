# Functioning

*Scope: physical and social activity in health assessment*

In health assessment, functioning is what a person is able to do in everyday physical and social life.

It includes activities such as moving, dressing, and interacting with others, with the relevant activities chosen for the assessment.

Functioning and felt well-being are related but distinct: a person can report symptoms or distress that an observer cannot infer from activity alone.
