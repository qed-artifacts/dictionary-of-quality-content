# Sensitivity

*Scope: sensitivity of a diagnostic test for a target condition*

For a diagnostic test, sensitivity is the proportion of people with the target condition who receive a positive result under a stated test rule.

It is the probability of a positive result given that the condition is present.

It is not the probability that someone with a positive result has the condition; that also depends on other factors, including how common the condition is in the tested population.
