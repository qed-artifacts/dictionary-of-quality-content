# Fact-checking

*Scope: verification of public factual claims in journalism*

In journalism, fact-checking is the examination of a checkable factual claim against available evidence to determine how well the claim holds up.

The checker identifies the precise claim, looks for relevant records or other independent evidence, and explains any finding in its context.

Repeating a claim or judging whether it sounds plausible is not itself a check of its factual basis.
