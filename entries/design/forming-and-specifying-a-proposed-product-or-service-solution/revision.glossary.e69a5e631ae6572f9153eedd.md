# Design

*Scope: forming and specifying a proposed product or service solution*

In quality management, design is the work of devising and specifying a solution for an identified purpose or requirement.

Its outputs can be plans, specifications, or prototypes that guide making a product or delivering a service.

A proposed design still needs checking against its inputs and intended use; having a specification alone does not show that the finished object works.
