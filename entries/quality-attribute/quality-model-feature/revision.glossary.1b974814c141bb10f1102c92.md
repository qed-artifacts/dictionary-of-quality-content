# Quality attribute

*Scope: quality-model feature*

A quality attribute is a named feature of an object treated as relevant to an account of its quality.

In systems and software work, a model may organize attributes such as reliability or performance efficiency into narrower attributes and connect them to measures.

Naming an attribute does not give its measured value or decide whether performance is good. The term overlaps with quality characteristic in some fields; specify the model and sense being used.
