# Externality

*Scope: effect on others outside an economic decision or exchange*

In economics, an externality is a benefit or harm from one party's activity that affects others without being fully accounted for in the decision or exchange that produced it.

Pollution affecting neighbors is a negative externality; an activity can also confer benefits on others.

Identify the affected parties and the boundary of the decision before calling an effect external.
