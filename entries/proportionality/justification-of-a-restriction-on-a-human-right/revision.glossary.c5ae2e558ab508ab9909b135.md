# Proportionality

*Scope: justification of a restriction on a human right*

In human-rights review, proportionality concerns whether a restriction on a right is justified by a legitimate aim in light of the burden it imposes.

The reviewer asks whether the interference is necessary and whether the competing rights and interests have been fairly balanced.

The precise test depends on the right and legal system; proportionality is not simply a numerical ratio.
