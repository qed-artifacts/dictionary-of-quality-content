# Design for quality

*Scope: anticipating quality concerns in product and process design*

Design for quality incorporates known and foreseeable quality concerns into the design of a product, process, or equipment before routine use.

Designers can turn those concerns into specifications, design choices, and checks intended to prevent failures.

Finding a defect later may prompt a redesign, but detection alone does not build prevention into the design.
