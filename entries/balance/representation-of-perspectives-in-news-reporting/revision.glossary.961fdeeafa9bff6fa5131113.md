# Balance

*Scope: representation of perspectives in news reporting*

In journalism, balance is the distribution of attention and representation among relevant sides or perspectives in a news account.

A balanced account may give each side a chance to be heard, but equal space or symmetrical quotations do not by themselves establish accuracy or fairness.

Judging balance therefore requires attention to what evidence supports each claim and which perspectives are relevant.
