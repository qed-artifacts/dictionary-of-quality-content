# Service quality

*Scope: service performance and user experience*

Service quality is how well a service performs in respects that matter to its users and its intended purpose.

The result delivered and the way the service is provided may both matter. A service judgment should identify whose experience is considered and the conditions under which the service was provided.

Expectation-gap and performance-only measures are different ways of studying perceived service quality. Neither formula is a universal definition of the service’s quality.
