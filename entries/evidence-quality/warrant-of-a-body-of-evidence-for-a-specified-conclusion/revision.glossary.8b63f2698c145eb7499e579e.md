# Evidence quality

*Scope: warrant of a body of evidence for a specified conclusion*

Evidence quality is the degree of confidence a body of evidence warrants for a specified question or conclusion.

Relevant concerns include how the evidence was produced, whether findings agree, how directly they address the question, and how precise they are. A strong individual study does not automatically make the whole body of evidence strong.

The quality of evidence and the strength of a recommendation are separate judgments: a decision also depends on benefits, harms, values, and context.
