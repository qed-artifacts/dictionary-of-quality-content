# Cutoff

*Scope: score boundary for test-based classification*

In test-based classification, a cutoff is a specified score boundary used to assign results to categories or trigger a decision.

For example, a proficiency cutoff separates scores reported as meeting a standard from those below it.

The score scale does not choose the boundary by itself; the cutoff and its consequences require a justified standard-setting decision.
