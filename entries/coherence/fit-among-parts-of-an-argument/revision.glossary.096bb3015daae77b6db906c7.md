# Coherence

*Scope: fit among parts of an argument*

In argumentation, coherence is the intelligible fit among an account's claims, reasons, and inferential links.

A reader should be able to follow how the parts belong together, including any unstated premises needed to connect them.

An argument can be coherent yet unpersuasive if its premises lack credible evidence or its support is insufficient.
