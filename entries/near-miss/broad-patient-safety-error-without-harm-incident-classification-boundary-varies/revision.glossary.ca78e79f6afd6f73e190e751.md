# Near miss

*Scope: broad patient-safety error without harm; incident-classification boundary varies*

In a broad patient-safety use, a near miss is an error that could have harmed a patient but did not, through interception or chance.

Some incident-classification systems reserve near miss for an incident that never reaches the patient and call one that reaches the patient without harm a no-harm incident.

Reviewing near misses can reveal hazards before they cause injury.
