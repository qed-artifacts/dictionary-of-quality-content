# Proxy

*Scope: observable stand-in for a less accessible evaluative construct*

In evaluation and measurement, a proxy is an observable measure used as a stand-in for a condition or construct that cannot be observed directly or readily.

Its usefulness depends on how well the measure tracks the intended target in the population and setting where it is used.

A change in the proxy need not equal the same change in the target; other influences and measurement error may affect the observed value.
