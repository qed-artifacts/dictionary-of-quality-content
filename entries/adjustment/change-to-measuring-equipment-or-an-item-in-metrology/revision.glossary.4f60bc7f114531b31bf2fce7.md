# Adjustment

*Scope: change to measuring equipment or an item in metrology*

In metrology, adjustment is a deliberate change to a measuring item or system that alters its output or indication.

It may bring an indication closer to a desired setting, but the change itself does not establish measurement accuracy.

Record the condition before and after adjustment when that difference matters; calibration and adjustment are distinct operations.
