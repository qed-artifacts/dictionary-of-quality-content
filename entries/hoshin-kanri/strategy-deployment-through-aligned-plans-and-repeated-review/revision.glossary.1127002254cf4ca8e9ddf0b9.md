# Hoshin kanri

*Scope: strategy deployment through aligned plans and repeated review*

Hoshin kanri is a method for turning an organization’s strategic priorities into aligned objectives and action plans across its levels.

Plans are developed and adjusted through dialogue among the people responsible for carrying them out. Responsibilities, resources, and measures make the priorities actionable.

Regular review connects results and lessons back to the next planning cycle. A statement of priorities alone is not a deployed plan.
