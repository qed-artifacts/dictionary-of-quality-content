# Decision

*Scope: choice of a course of action*

A decision is a choice among possible courses of action, including whether to act, defer, or seek more information.

A defensible decision identifies the options, the aim, the relevant evidence, and the likely consequences and uncertainties. A judgment or evaluation can inform the choice without uniquely determining it.

The decision is the choice made; its supporting inference, criteria, and evidence are distinct parts of its justification.
