# Deviation

*Scope: observation's difference from a statistical reference*

In statistics, a deviation is the difference between an observed value and a specified reference value, often the mean of a data set.

A signed deviation preserves direction; its absolute value gives the size of the difference without direction.

A deviation from a mean is not, by itself, evidence that a requirement was violated; that requires a separate decision rule.
