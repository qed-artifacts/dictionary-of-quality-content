# Trustworthiness

*Scope: grounds for relying on a system to meet critical requirements*

In systems engineering, trustworthiness is the extent to which a system warrants reliance on it to fulfill specified critical requirements under relevant conditions.

Those requirements may concern safety, security, reliability, performance, or resilience, depending on the system and its use.

A claim of trustworthiness needs evidence for the relevant requirements; success on one attribute does not establish all the others.
