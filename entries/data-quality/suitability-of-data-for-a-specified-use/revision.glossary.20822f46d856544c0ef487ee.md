# Data quality

*Scope: suitability of data for a specified use*

Data quality is how well data are suited to a specified use and its requirements.

Aspects such as accuracy, completeness, consistency, and currency can matter, but their relevance depends on the data, question, and setting. A useful assessment says which aspect was checked and how.

Data that support one decision may be inadequate for another. A quality dimension names a concern; a metric or check specifies how evidence about that concern is obtained.
