# Six Sigma

*Scope: process-improvement family and statistical capability use*

Six Sigma is a family of data-guided methods for improving processes by reducing unwanted variation and defects.

Improvement projects define a problem, examine how the process performs, test causes, make changes, and monitor whether gains persist. Different roadmaps apply to an existing process and to a new design.

The phrase also has a statistical use concerning a process’s modeled performance. A claimed sigma level needs a stated defect definition, opportunity unit, measurement method, and calculation assumptions; the name alone does not demonstrate performance.
