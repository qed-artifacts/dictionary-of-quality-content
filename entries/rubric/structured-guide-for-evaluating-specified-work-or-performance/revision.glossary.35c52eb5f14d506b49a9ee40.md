# Rubric

*Scope: structured guide for evaluating specified work or performance*

A rubric is a structured guide for judging a specified kind of work or performance against stated criteria.

It may describe levels of performance, give examples, assign points, or set out scoring rules. These details help reviewers apply the criteria and make the basis of a judgment visible.

A rubric organizes judgment; it does not establish that the criteria are appropriate or that different reviewers will apply them consistently. Those questions require separate justification and checking.
