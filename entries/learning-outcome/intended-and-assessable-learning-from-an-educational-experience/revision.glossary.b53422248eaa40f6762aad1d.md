# Learning outcome

*Scope: intended and assessable learning from an educational experience*

An intended learning outcome states what a learner is expected to know, understand, or be able to demonstrate after an educational experience.

It describes learning by the student rather than only material covered or an activity performed by the instructor.

The phrase can also name what learners actually achieved, so an assessment should distinguish the intended outcome from evidence of attainment.
