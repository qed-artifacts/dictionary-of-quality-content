# Construction quality

*Scope: project work and built asset against applicable requirements*

Construction quality is how well the design and construction work, and the resulting asset, meet applicable project requirements and intended performance.

Requirements may concern materials, workmanship, completed characteristics, and performance in use. They must be tied to the particular project and contract.

Passing one inspection establishes only what that check covered, not the quality of the whole asset.
