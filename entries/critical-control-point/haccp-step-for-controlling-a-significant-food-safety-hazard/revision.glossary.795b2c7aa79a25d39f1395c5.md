# Critical control point

*Scope: HACCP step for controlling a significant food-safety hazard*

In food-safety HACCP, a critical control point is a step where control is necessary to prevent, eliminate, or reduce a significant hazard to an acceptable level.

A HACCP plan identifies the point, a limit for the control, and how that limit will be monitored.

Not every routine production check is a critical control point; the designation depends on the hazard and the control available at that step.
