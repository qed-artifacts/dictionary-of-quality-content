# Floor effect

*Scope: lower-end limit in health-status measurement*

A floor effect occurs when a measure's lower limit prevents it from distinguishing values or changes below that limit.

When many respondents receive the lowest available score, their underlying states may still differ even though the instrument reports the same value.

A low score alone does not establish a floor effect; the concern is lost discrimination at the lower end of the scale.
