# Internal consistency

*Scope: coherence among items in a multi-item measure*

In psychometrics, internal consistency is how strongly items in a multi-item measure relate as indicators of the intended construct.

Split-half methods and coefficients such as Cronbach's alpha summarize aspects of that relationship, and test length can affect the result.

A high internal-consistency estimate provides reliability evidence, not proof that the measure is valid for a particular interpretation or use.
