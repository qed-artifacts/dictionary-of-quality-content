# Quality measurement

*Scope: obtaining values for specified quality-related quantities*

Quality measurement is the use of a specified procedure to obtain values for quantities that bear on a stated quality question.

Identify what is measured, the subject and conditions, the method, and the resulting value. A quality model or requirement explains why that quantity matters.

The measurement result is evidence. Deciding whether the result is good, sufficient, or compliant requires a further interpretive rule or judgment.
