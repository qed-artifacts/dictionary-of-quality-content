# Specificity

*Scope: diagnostic-test classification of people without the target condition*

In diagnostic testing, specificity is the proportion of people without the target condition who test negative.

It shows how well the test avoids false positives among people who do not have the condition.

It is not the probability that a person with a negative result is free of the condition; that is negative predictive value.
