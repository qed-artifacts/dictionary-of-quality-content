# Privacy

*Scope: personal information in digital systems*

In digital information systems, privacy concerns appropriate protection against unwanted collection, use, disclosure, or inference of information about people.

What is appropriate depends on the information, its purpose, the people affected, and the setting.

Security safeguards can help, but even securely held data may be used in ways that violate reasonable privacy expectations.
