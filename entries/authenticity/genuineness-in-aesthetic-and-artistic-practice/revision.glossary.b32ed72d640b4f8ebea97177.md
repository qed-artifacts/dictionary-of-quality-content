# Authenticity

*Scope: genuineness in aesthetic and artistic practice*

In aesthetic practice, authenticity is an appraisal that a work, performance, or experience is genuine in a respect that matters to that practice.

The relevant respect may concern its origins, recognizable features, or the maker's expression; these are distinct accounts rather than one universal test.

An authenticity claim should state the respect and practice at issue. Authenticity alone does not settle overall merit, and its standards can be applied unfairly.
