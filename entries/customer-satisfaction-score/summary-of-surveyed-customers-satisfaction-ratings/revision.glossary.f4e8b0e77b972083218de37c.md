# Customer satisfaction score

*Scope: summary of surveyed customers' satisfaction ratings*

A customer satisfaction score is a summary value calculated from customers' ratings of a specified product, service, or interaction.

It may be reported as an average rating or as the proportion choosing designated satisfied categories; the question, scale, cutoff, and sample determine its meaning.

The score reports responses under those conditions, not an objective verdict on every aspect of service quality.
