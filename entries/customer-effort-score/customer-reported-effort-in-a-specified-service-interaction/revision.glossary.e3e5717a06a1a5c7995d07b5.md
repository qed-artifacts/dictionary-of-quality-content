# Customer effort score

*Scope: customer-reported effort in a specified service interaction*

Customer effort score is a survey-based metric summarizing how much effort customers report needing to complete a specified interaction with an organization.

A useful score states the question, response scale, interaction, population, and method for combining responses.

Less reported effort may indicate an easier interaction, but the score alone does not establish overall service quality.
