# Customer value

*Scope: customer-perceived worth of an offering in a use setting*

In marketing, customer value is a customer's appraisal of how an offering serves what matters to them in a particular use setting.

The appraisal can involve functional, economic, social, or emotional considerations, depending on the customer and the choice.

A supplier's claim about value does not determine what the customer actually values.
