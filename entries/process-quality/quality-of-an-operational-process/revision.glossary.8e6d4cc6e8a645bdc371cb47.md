# Process quality

*Scope: quality of an operational process*

Process quality is how well a process is designed and carried out to meet requirements for the work it performs.

A judgment should identify the process, its purpose, and the relevant conditions, controls, and performance criteria. Monitoring variation and feedback can show whether it remains fit for that purpose.

Process quality and output quality are related but distinct. One acceptable output does not establish that the process is consistently controlled, and a controlled process still requires checks on what it produces.
