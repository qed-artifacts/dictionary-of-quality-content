# Ecological validity

*Scope: applicability of research findings to real-life situations*

In empirical research, ecological validity concerns how well findings from a study setting apply to the real-life situations the study aims to represent.

It draws attention to the relation between study tasks and surroundings and the corresponding activities outside the study.

Realistic conditions alone do not establish that results generalize to every population or setting.
