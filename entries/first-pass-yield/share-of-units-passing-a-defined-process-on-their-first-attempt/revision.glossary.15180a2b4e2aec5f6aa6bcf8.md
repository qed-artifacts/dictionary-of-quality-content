# First-pass yield

*Scope: share of units passing a defined process on their first attempt*

First-pass yield is the proportion of units entering a specified process step that meet its acceptance criteria on the first attempt, without rework or retesting.

Count first-pass conforming units over all eligible units entering the step. State the step, unit, population, and time period so the result can be compared meaningfully.

A unit repaired and accepted later may contribute to final output, but it did not pass on the first attempt. For a multistep flow, a separate rolled yield addresses passing every step without rework.
