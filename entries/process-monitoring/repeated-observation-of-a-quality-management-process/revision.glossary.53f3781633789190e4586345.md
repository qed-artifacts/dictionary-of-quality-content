# Process monitoring

*Scope: repeated observation of a quality-management process*

Process monitoring is the repeated observation or measurement of selected process conditions and results to see how a process is behaving over time.

Useful indicators relate to the process’s objectives and have a stated collection method and frequency. Trends or departures can prompt investigation and action.

Monitoring supplies evidence for decisions; it does not by itself control the process or prove that every output conforms.
