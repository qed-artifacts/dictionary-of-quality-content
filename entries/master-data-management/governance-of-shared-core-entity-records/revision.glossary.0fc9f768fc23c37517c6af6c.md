# Master data management

*Scope: governance of shared core entity records*

Master data management is the coordinated governance of shared records for core entities, such as customers or products, so their identities and key attributes remain consistent across an organization.

It addresses conflicting versions of the same entity in different systems or business units.

Master data describe enduring entities; transaction data record events such as orders or payments.
