# Quality management maturity

*Scope: appraisal of pharmaceutical manufacturing-site quality-management practice*

For a pharmaceutical manufacturing site, quality management maturity is an appraisal of how developed and consistently applied its quality-management practices are under a specified assessment framework.

The framework determines which practices and evidence count and how maturity is characterized.

A maturity assessment concerns the management system; it does not directly measure the quality of every product the site makes.
