# Hazard rate

*Scope: instantaneous conditional failure rate in reliability*

In reliability analysis, the hazard rate at a specified age is the instantaneous failure rate among items that have survived to that age.

It relates failures near that age to the items still operating; for a continuous lifetime model, the rate equals the lifetime density divided by the survival probability.

A hazard rate has units of inverse time or another exposure unit and is not itself the probability of failure over an interval.
