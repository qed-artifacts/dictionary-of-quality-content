# Accreditation

*Scope: higher-education institutional or program recognition*

In higher education, accreditation is an accreditor's public recognition of an institution or program based on its standards and requirements.

The recognition applies to the institution or program named by the accreditor; a program’s status does not automatically extend to its whole institution.

Accreditation can carry findings or monitoring. Name the accreditor and the status before treating the recognition as evidence that every requirement is fully met.
