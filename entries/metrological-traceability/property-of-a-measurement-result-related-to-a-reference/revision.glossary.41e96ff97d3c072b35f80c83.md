# Metrological traceability

*Scope: property of a measurement result related to a reference*

Metrological traceability is the property of a measurement result that lets it be related to a reference through a documented, unbroken chain of calibrations, each contributing to measurement uncertainty.

The chain identifies how the result connects to the reference and where uncertainty enters. It belongs to a particular result and measurement process, rather than merely to an instrument label.

Traceability makes the basis for comparison inspectable. It does not by itself prove that the result is accurate enough for a particular decision; that requires an uncertainty and fitness-for-use judgment.
